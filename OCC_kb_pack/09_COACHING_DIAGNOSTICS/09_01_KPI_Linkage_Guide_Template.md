## Key Insights for Coaching

* **Biggest Lever Behaviors:** Pre-Close mastery, strong Price Conditioning, and Urgency in Closing Sequence have the highest impact on Same-Day Close %.
* **Average Ticket Drivers:** Cost vs Value, Hands-on Demo, and Sharp Angle technique.
* **Financing Usage:** Directly tied to ROI discussion and monthly payment isolation.
* **Demo/Show Rate:** Heavily influenced by Warm-Up and Product Demo quality.
