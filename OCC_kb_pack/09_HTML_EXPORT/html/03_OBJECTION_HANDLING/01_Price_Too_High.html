Price Too High

Customer Says

  • "That's too much."
  • "That is more than I expected."
  • "I did not think it would be that expensive."
  • "Your price is high."
  • "I cannot afford that."

What It Usually Means

The customer may mean one of three things:

  1. They do not understand the full value yet.
  2. They understand the value but the payment does not fit.
  3. They are testing whether the rep will panic-discount.

Do not assume price is the real issue until it is isolated.

Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System

Primary steps:

  • Step 4 - Investment / Price Conditioning
  • Step 5 - Product Demonstration
  • Step 8 - Price Presentation

If this objection appears in Step 8, the rep must stay inside the approved Step 8 payment-control sequence.

Approved Response Framework

Acknowledge

I hear you. The full amount does look big at first.

Redirect to Monthly

That is exactly why we look at monthly options.

Isolate Payment

What monthly investment would feel comfortable for you on a project like this?

Confirm Target

Got it. You want your payments to be at $___ per month. So, for me or anyone else to earn your business we would need to be at $___ per month or less. Is that correct?

Trial Close List Price

If we can get your payment at $___ can we earn your business at $List Price?

Close-Back Question

If we can make the monthly investment fit, are you comfortable moving forward with the full project today?

KPI Impact

  • Margin
  • Financing Attachment
  • Close Rate
  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Discount Discipline

Do Not Say

  • "Let me see what I can do."
  • "I can probably get my manager to drop it."
  • "What if I gave you a discount?"
  • "This is the cheapest we can do" unless it is approved and true.
  • "You will definitely be approved."

AI Coach Note

If the rep responds to price by discounting before isolating payment, score Step 8 no higher than 2.

Correct the rep back to payment isolation before promotion.

Practice Drill

Have the rep run Step 8 from List Price through payment confirmation three times without offering a discount.
