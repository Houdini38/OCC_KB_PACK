Don't Want Financing

Customer Says

  • "We do not want financing."
  • "We do not finance things."
  • "We prefer to pay cash."
  • "I do not want another loan."
  • "I do not want my credit pulled."

What It Usually Means

This may mean:

  1. The customer truly prefers cash.
  2. The customer does not understand the purpose of monthly framing.
  3. The customer is worried about credit.
  4. The customer does not want debt.
  5. The customer is resisting the total investment.
  6. The customer may still need a payment option but does not want to say so.

Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System

Primary step:

  • Step 8 - Price Presentation

This must stay inside Step 8 and financing compliance rules.

Approved Response Framework

Acknowledge

I understand. Some homeowners prefer not to finance.

Clarify Ability to Move Forward

If you do not want to use our financing, are you comfortable putting 35% down and paying the balance upon completion?

If Yes

Proceed with the cash/deposit path if all other objections are cleared.

If No or Hesitant

Return to payment isolation without promising financing approval.

I understand. That is exactly why we look at monthly options. What monthly investment would feel comfortable for you on a project like this?

Close-Back Question

If the payment structure works in a way you are comfortable with, are you comfortable moving forward with the project today?

KPI Impact

  • Financing Attachment
  • Margin
  • Close Rate
  • Same-Day Close Rate

Do Not Say

  • "You should finance it."
  • "Everyone finances these projects."
  • "You will be approved."
  • "Your credit will be fine."
  • "It will not affect your credit."
  • "We can get you approved."
  • "This is free money."

Compliance Guardrail

Financing is available for qualified customers. The lender determines approval and terms.

Do not promise approval, payment, APR, term, or credit outcome.

AI Coach Note

If the customer refuses financing, the rep must verify whether they can pay the 35% deposit and balance upon completion before moving forward.

If the rep ignores this checkpoint, score Step 8 lower.

Practice Drill

Have the rep respond to "I do not want financing" without arguing and without promising approval.
