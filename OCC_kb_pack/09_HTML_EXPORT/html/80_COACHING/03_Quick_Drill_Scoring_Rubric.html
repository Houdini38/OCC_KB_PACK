Quick Drill Scoring Rubric

Purpose

This rubric controls short-form drill scoring.

Use this rubric for:

  • Objection drills
  • Step 6 Pre-Close isolation drills
  • Step 8 payment-isolation drills
  • Step 9 final-objection drills
  • Follow-up call micro-drills when a point score is requested

Do not use this rubric for full roleplay sessions. Use 80_COACHING/00_Roleplay_Scoring_Rubric.md for full roleplays.

Quick Drill Scoring Categories

Total score: 100 points.

# Category Points What It Measures
1 Acknowledges calmly 10 No defensiveness, no rushing past it
2 Isolates root concern 20 Diagnoses before answering
3 Uses approved system structure 20 Follows approved sequence and wording where locked
4 Ties back to homeowner problem 15 Connects response to inspection, urgency, or stated need
5 Uses urgency / incentive correctly 15 No fake scarcity, no invented promotion
6 Closes back clearly 10 Asks for decision or defined next step
7 Delivery 10 Confident, concise, no filler spiral
Total 100

Category 1 - Acknowledges Calmly

Score Standard
10 Calm, brief acknowledgment.
7 Acknowledged but rushed past it.
4 Defensive or argumentative.
0 Skipped acknowledgment.

Category 2 - Isolates Root Concern

Score Standard
20 Fully isolates the real issue before responding.
15 Good isolation but missed one possible branch.
10 Vague isolation question.
5 Assumed the objection without asking.
0 Skipped isolation.

Approved isolation examples:

Is it the product, the company, the timing, or the investment?
Is the concern the total investment or the monthly payment?

Category 3 - Approved System Structure

Score Standard
20 Uses approved structure and locked language where required.
15 Close paraphrase with correct structure.
10 Right idea but weak or out-of-order delivery.
5 Improvised outside the system.
0 Compliance violation or prohibited claim.

Automatic zero for this category if the rep:

  • Guarantees financing approval
  • Says "free financing" or "no-cost financing"
  • Invents APR, term, payment, warranty, product spec, ROI, or manager authority
  • Skips required deferred-interest disclosure
  • Uses fake urgency

Category 4 - Tie Back

Score Standard
15 Specific tie-back to inspection findings, Past-Future-Present, or customer words.
10 Generic tie-back to the project.
5 No meaningful tie-back.
0 Contradicts earlier customer facts or inspection findings.

Category 5 - Urgency / Incentive

Score Standard
15 Uses approved urgency, Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity, or payment logic correctly.
10 Uses general urgency safely but not powerfully.
5 Vague urgency.
0 Invented promo, fake deadline, or unapproved discount authority.

Category 6 - Close Back

Score Standard
10 Direct ask for decision or specific next action.
7 Asked, but weakly.
4 Trailed off and waited for customer to volunteer.
0 No close-back question.

Category 7 - Delivery

Score Standard
10 Confident, concise, no filler spiral.
7 One or two small delivery issues.
4 Stumbled or overexplained.
0 Repeated false starts or confusing delivery.

Score Ranges

Total Tier Hope's Next Step
90-100 Table-Ready Move to harder customer or pressure modifier.
80-89 Close One targeted correction, then repeat three times.
70-79 Needs Work Drill the lowest category separately.
60-69 Foundation Gap Return to Coaching Mode on the weakest category.
Below 60 Upstream Failure Diagnose Step 2, Step 4, Step 6, or Step 8 before more objection scripts.

Coaching Tag Output

Hope should produce this structured tag when scoring is requested:

Rep:
Drill:
Total Score: __ / 100
Lowest Category:
Main Weakness:
Sales System Area:
KPI Impact:
Assignment:
Manager Alert: Yes / No

Manager Alert should be Yes if:

  • Same weakness appears three sessions in a row
  • Any Tier 1 compliance issue repeats twice in one session
  • Rep refuses approved system correction

Final Rule

Quick Drill scoring rewards clean, repeatable behavior.

Do not let a clever answer outrank correct sequence.
