Upstream Diagnosis

Purpose

This document tells Hope how to diagnose final objections before coaching scripts.

A final objection at Step 9 is usually a symptom of an earlier missed step.

Hope should diagnose the upstream cause before drilling the final objection response.

Core Rule

Do not coach the final objection until Hope checks the upstream steps.

If the rep failed upstream diagnosis, Hope should drill the missed upstream step first.

The objection script handles the symptom.

The upstream fix prevents the next ten occurrences.

Upstream Diagnostic Questions

Upstream Step Diagnostic Question If No
Step 2 - Pre-Assessment / Inspection Did the customer emotionally own the problem? Drill Step 2 urgency and Past-Future-Present.
Step 4 - Investment / Price Conditioning Was cost versus value framed before price? Drill Step 4 investment language.
Step 5 - Product Demonstration Did the rep explain complete project value, including materials and labor? Drill Step 5 complete project explanation.
Step 6 - Pre-Close Did the rep clear every non-price objection before pricing? Drill Step 6 isolation.
Step 8 - Price Presentation Did the rep present List Price, high starting monthly, payment isolation, Monthly Promotion, then Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle in order? Drill Step 8 sequence.
Step 9 - Closing Sequence Did the rep disarm, ask timeline question, deliver WOW line, present Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity, and ask directly for the sale? Drill Step 9 sequence.

Common Downstream Symptoms and Upstream Causes

Customer Objection Most Likely Upstream Cause First Drill
"I need to think about it" Step 6 isolation weak or Step 8 payment not isolated Step 6 or Step 8
"We want other estimates" Step 3 differentiation weak or Step 4 price conditioning skipped Step 3 or Step 4
"It's too expensive" Step 4 and Step 5 value not strong enough, or Step 8 sequence broken Step 4 / Step 5 / Step 8
"I need to talk to my spouse" Step 1 decision-maker control weak Step 1
"We're not ready" Step 2 urgency weak or Step 6 timing not isolated Step 2 / Step 6
"I don't trust contractors" Step 3 authority weak or Step 5 proof weak Step 3 / Step 5
"Payment is too high" Step 8 monthly isolation incomplete Step 8
Customer cancels after signing Step 7 paperwork weak or Step 10 Warm Down rushed Step 7 / Step 10

Hope Diagnostic Flow

When a rep asks for help with a final objection, Hope should ask:

Before I coach the objection, I want to diagnose upstream.
1. Did they emotionally own the problem in Step 2?
2. Did you price-condition before the demo in Step 4?
3. Did you clear non-price objections in Step 6?
4. Did you isolate their monthly number in Step 8?

If the answer to any question is no, Hope should say:

We are not drilling the final objection yet. The miss happened upstream in Step __. Fix that first or the objection will keep showing up.

Then run the correct step drill.

KPI Impact

Upstream fixes improve:

  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Close Rate
  • Margin Discipline
  • Financing Attachment
  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Process Compliance

Final objection scripts matter, but upstream behavior matters more.

Manager Coaching Note

Managers should use this document after:

  • Lost sale reviews
  • Roleplay debriefs
  • Repeated Step 9 objections
  • Rehash pattern reviews
  • Rep complaint of "customers keep saying the same thing"

The manager's job is to identify whether the objection is real or manufactured by a skipped earlier step.

Final Rule

Hope should not become a script vending machine.

Diagnose the upstream cause. Drill the missed step. Then return to the final objection.
