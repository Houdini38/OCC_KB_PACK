Rep FAQ — Internal Questions

Purpose

Common questions reps ask Hope during coaching or training.

FAQ files do not override higher-authority sales process, financing, warranty, product, or coaching documents.

On the System

Why do we have to do all 10 steps?

The system is built for the broad case. Each step eliminates a specific objection. Skip a step and the matching objection usually returns at the close.

Can I shortcut with an easy customer?

Only when the customer type and appointment clearly call for efficient delivery. The system protects reps against customers who look easy but are not.

What if the customer just wants a price?

Use approved expectation-setting language. The rep should explain that exact pricing requires inspection, scope, and options first.

On Discounts

Can I offer a discount to close?

Use only approved promotion language and approved authority. Do not volunteer a discount before Step 8 payment isolation and the approved Step 9 close sequence.

What if my manager said I can drop more?

Confirm with the manager. One-off approval is not a new default script.

Can I match a competitor's quote?

Investigate first. Compare scope, materials, warranty, install method, and total project details before discussing any match.

On Financing

Should I always offer financing?

Always mention financing in Step 8. Do not push if the customer is a confirmed cash buyer. Use financing as an affordability tool, not a discount substitute.

What if they are declined?

Do not invent a workaround. Use the approved financing documents and manager guidance.

What if they ask about a financing plan we do not offer?

Use the missing-information phrase:

I am not sure that is a plan we currently offer. Let me confirm with our team and follow up.

Never invent a plan.

On Roleplays

How often should I roleplay?

Weekly minimum. New reps should roleplay more frequently during onboarding.

Can I bring real customer notes to a roleplay?

Yes. Real customer notes make roleplay more useful.

What is a passing score?

3.5+ overall is acceptable. 4.0+ is strong. Anything under 3.0 should trigger a focused drill on the weakest dimension.

Difficulty labels are Easy, Medium, and Hard.

On Customer-Risk Language

What if I accidentally say something wrong?

Correct yourself and continue. If the mistake materially misleads the customer, stop and restate it cleanly.

What if a customer asks me a question I do not know?

Use the approved phrase:

That is a great question. I do not want to guess on something that could affect your decision. Let me get the verified answer from our team and follow up. Is that fair?

Do not invent an answer.

What if my manager tells me to say something not in the KB?

Confirm in writing. If it is a policy update, the KB needs to be updated. If it is a one-off approval, do not generalize from it.

On Hope

Can I ask Hope anything?

Use Hope for sales training, product, financing, warranty, objection handling, roleplay, and coaching.

Does Hope tell my manager what I asked?

Hope sessions may be reviewed to improve coaching quality.

What if Hope is wrong?

Flag the issue through the change process. The KB gets updated, and Hope gets better.

On Career

How do I get promoted?

Speak with your manager about leadership opportunities and the KPIs required.

What KPIs are tracked?

See 08_Coaching/02_KPI_Definitions.md.

How does my pay work?

Compensation specifics are handled by HR or management. Hope should not speculate.

Final Rule

Hope is a sales coach, not a generalist assistant.
