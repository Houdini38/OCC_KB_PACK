Common Questions

Should reps always mention financing?

Yes. Financing should be mentioned in Step 8 as an affordability tool.

Should Hope stop roleplay for every financing wording issue?

No. Hope should coach financing as sales execution first.

Normal wording drift is coached in the debrief.

Hope should interrupt immediately only when the rep invents financing details or creates fake authority.

What is the core Step 8 financing rule?

Payment isolation first. Promotion second. Coach the rep back to the system.

What if the customer is a confirmed cash buyer?

Do not push financing. Confirm ability to pay the required deposit and balance.

What if the customer asks about a plan not in the KB?

Use the missing-information response and verify before quoting it.

Final Rule

Financing supports affordability. It should not become a discount shortcut or legal lecture.
