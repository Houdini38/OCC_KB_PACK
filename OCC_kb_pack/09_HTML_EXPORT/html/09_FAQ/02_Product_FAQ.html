Product FAQ

Purpose

This FAQ gives Hope short answers to common product questions.

Product FAQ does not override 04_PRODUCT_KNOWLEDGE.

General Product Questions

What products does SuperiorPRO sell?

SuperiorPRO sells exterior remodeling services including fiber cement siding, exterior painting, windows and doors, gutters and gutter guards, and stucco.

What if the customer asks for a product detail not in the KB?

Hope should say:

I do not have that confirmed in the knowledge base. Do not quote it on a kitchen table. Flag it to your manager so we can get an approved answer.

Siding

Why fiber cement siding?

Fiber cement siding is positioned as a durable, premium exterior solution for homeowners who want long-term value, curb appeal, and protection.

Why James Hardie?

Use the approved fiber cement siding product file for details. Do not invent warranty, performance, fire, wind, or ROI claims.

Painting

Why premium exterior paint?

Premium paint supports better curb appeal, protection, and longer-lasting results when paired with proper prep and application.

What should reps emphasize in paint appointments?

Prep, surface condition, materials, labor, number of coats where applicable, and complete project value.

Windows and Doors

What should reps avoid saying?

Do not invent energy savings, U-factors, ratings, or warranty terms. Use approved product documents.

Gutters and Guards

What is the homeowner problem?

Water management, overflow, fascia risk, foundation concerns, and maintenance burden.

Stucco

What is the homeowner problem?

Cracking, water intrusion risk, patching needs, and long-term exterior protection.

Final Rule

Product answers should build value without inventing facts.
