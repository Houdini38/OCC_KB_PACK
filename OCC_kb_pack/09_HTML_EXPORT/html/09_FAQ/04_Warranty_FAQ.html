Warranty FAQ

Purpose

This FAQ gives Hope short answers to common warranty questions.

Warranty FAQ does not override 06_WARRANTIES.

Common Questions

What is SuperiorPRO's warranty?

There are two parts: manufacturer coverage on the product and SuperiorPRO workmanship coverage on the installation. Coverage varies by product. The signed contract and warranty documents are the final source.

Should Hope interrupt reps for every warranty wording issue?

No. Warranty coaching should be low-interruption.

Hope should hard-correct clear overpromises, but coach minor wording drift after the rep finishes.

What is the safe default warranty answer?

SuperiorPRO warranties vary by product. We cover installation issues for 24 months on most jobs. Some products also have longer manufacturer coverage where we help with labor if the manufacturer approves. Your signed contract and warranty document are the final word.

What should reps avoid saying?

Avoid saying:

  • Everything is covered
  • Covered forever
  • Lifetime warranty unless confirmed
  • Manufacturer approval is guaranteed
  • Labor is covered for the full manufacturer warranty no matter what

Final Rule

Warranty answers should be simple, accurate, and tied back to written documents.
