Customer FAQ

Purpose

Anticipated questions from homeowners. Hope uses this for common homeowner questions and as inspiration for roleplay objections.

FAQ files do not override higher-authority product, warranty, financing, or sales-process files.

Company / Trust

How long has SuperiorPRO been in business?

SuperiorPRO was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Kennesaw, Georgia.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. SuperiorPRO carries general liability and workers' compensation coverage. A certificate of insurance can be provided on request.

What is your BBB rating?

SuperiorPRO maintains an A+ BBB rating.

Can I see references?

Absolutely. We can provide local references and examples from recent projects.

Do you use subcontractors or employees?

SuperiorPRO uses certified installers designated as subcontractors for tax purposes. They are vetted, many have worked with SuperiorPRO for years, and project managers are SuperiorPRO employees.

Process / Install

How long will the project take?

It depends on service line and project size.

Typical ranges:

  • Full siding on a 2,000 sq ft home: often 5 to 10 days
  • Windows: often 30 to 60 minutes per window
  • Paint: often 3 to 7 days

The rep should give the exact timeline after inspection and scope confirmation.

Will I need to be home?

Not the entire time. We coordinate access at the start and end of the project and work around the homeowner's schedule where possible.

What about landscaping, pets, and kids?

SuperiorPRO plans around access points, debris management, plant protection, pets, and family routines. The rep should ask about specific concerns during the appointment.

What if it rains during the project?

SuperiorPRO monitors weather. Exterior work is scheduled around conditions so quality is protected.

Who do I call if there is an issue during the project?

The homeowner will have access to the project manager. Project managers work to respond quickly and visit job sites as needed.

Materials

Why James Hardie, Sherwin-Williams, Simonton, or ProVia?

SuperiorPRO partners with manufacturers it trusts for long-term performance. Product choice should be explained using approved product knowledge files.

Why not vinyl siding?

Vinyl is usually cheaper upfront, but fiber cement is positioned as the stronger long-term value when the homeowner wants durability, curb appeal, and a premium exterior solution.

What colors are available?

Color options vary by product. Hardie offers ColorPlus options, Sherwin-Williams offers a large paint color library, and some product lines may offer custom-match options. Reps should verify exact product-specific color availability before promising it.

Warranty

What is your warranty?

There are two parts: manufacturer coverage on the product and SuperiorPRO workmanship coverage on the installation. Coverage varies by product. The signed contract and warranty documents are the final source.

Is your warranty transferable?

Transferability depends on the product and warranty type. Reps should use the approved warranty files and avoid guessing.

What is not covered?

Common exclusions may include owner modifications, acts of nature beyond product ratings, pre-existing structural issues, or third-party work after installation. The written warranty documents control the final answer.

Pricing / Financing

How much will this cost?

It depends on scope, measurements, and selections. The rep should provide exact pricing after inspection and project review, not a loose ballpark.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. SuperiorPRO offers financing options. The rep should use the approved financing documents and present financing as an affordability tool, not a discount substitute.

What is the interest rate?

SuperiorPRO has multiple plan options. The rep should use the approved financing documents and avoid quoting unverified terms.

Will the credit check hurt my score?

The rep should answer from the active financing documents or confirm with the financing team if unsure. Do not guess on credit-process details.

Do you offer discounts?

SuperiorPRO may have seasonal promotions and the Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity for homeowners ready to move forward today. Reps should follow the approved Step 8 and Step 9 sequence.

Can you match a competitor's price?

The rep should first compare scope, material grade, warranty, installation method, and crew. Competitor price is not meaningful until the quote is compared line by line.

Decision / Timing

Why should I do this now?

The rep should tie urgency to the inspection findings, the homeowner's stated problem, and any current approved offer. Do not use fake scarcity.

Can I think about it overnight?

The rep should isolate what the homeowner needs to think through and answer it if possible. Do not accept a vague stall without understanding the real concern.

Do you do free estimates?

Yes. The appointment is no charge, and the homeowner should receive exact pricing after the inspection and review.
