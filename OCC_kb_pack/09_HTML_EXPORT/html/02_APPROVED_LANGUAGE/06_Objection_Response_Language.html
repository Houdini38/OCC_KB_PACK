Objection Response Language

Purpose

This file defines the structure for approved objection responses.

Objection responses must protect the 10-step sales system. They should not become random comeback lines.

This file is a framework. Specific objection files in 03_OBJECTION_HANDLING will contain the detailed scenario responses.

Core Rule

Do not answer an objection before isolating it.

A vague objection usually hides the real issue.

The agent should coach the rep to slow down, isolate the concern, connect it back to the correct step, and ask a close-back or next-step question.

Approved Objection Response Structure

Use this structure:

Acknowledge:
Isolate:
Clarify:
Respond:
Tie Back:
Close Back:

1. Acknowledge

Acknowledge without agreeing that the objection is final.

Example:

I understand. That makes sense to ask.

2. Isolate

Find the real concern.

Example:

When you say you need to think about it, what specifically do you need to think through: the project, SuperiorPRO, the timing, or the investment?

3. Clarify

Make the customer explain the objection in their own words.

Example:

Tell me more about that. What part feels unresolved?

4. Respond

Answer the real concern using approved sales system, product, financing, warranty, or compliance information.

5. Tie Back

Reconnect to what the customer already agreed to earlier in the appointment.

Example:

Earlier, you said protecting the home before this gets worse was the main reason you called. That is why I do not want to leave this vague.

6. Close Back

Ask for a decision, a next step, or a specific action.

Example:

If we can solve that concern, are you comfortable moving forward today?

Objection Placement Rule

The agent must identify where the objection belongs in the 10-step system.

Objection Type Likely Missed Step
"I need to think about it" Step 6, Step 8, or Step 9
"I want three prices" Step 3 or Step 4
"Too expensive" Step 4, Step 5, or Step 8
"I need to talk to my spouse" Step 1 or Step 6
"Not ready" Step 2 or Step 6
"I do not want financing" Step 8 / Financing Explanation
"I do not trust contractors" Step 3
"Call me later" Step 6, Step 8, or Follow Up Call Mode

What Not To Do

Do not:

  • Argue with the customer.
  • Overcome before isolating.
  • Discount before understanding.
  • Use pressure when the issue is trust.
  • Use more product information when the issue is decision-maker absence.
  • Use Step 9 Promo Home language before Step 9.
  • Promise financing approval.
  • Invent new promotional authority.
  • Make unsupported product or warranty claims.

KPI Impact

Objection handling affects:

  • Close Rate
  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Margin
  • Financing Attachment
  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Process Compliance

Coaching Note

When a rep fails on an objection, Hope should diagnose whether the objection was real or created by a missed earlier step.

Example:

The objection sounded like price, but the real breakdown was Step 5. The homeowner never understood the complete project, so the price felt unsupported.
