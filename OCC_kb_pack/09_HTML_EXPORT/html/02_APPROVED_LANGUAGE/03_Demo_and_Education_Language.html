Demo and Education Language

Purpose

This file controls approved demo and education language for Step 5 - Product Demonstration.

The product demonstration should turn spoken value into physical proof.

This file supports the Sales System Master and product knowledge files. If this file conflicts with approved product knowledge, the product knowledge file wins.

Core Rule

Do not show samples like a science fair.

The rep must explain the complete project:

  • Materials
  • Prep
  • Removal
  • Repairs
  • Installation
  • Labor
  • Protection
  • Cleanup
  • Documentation
  • Warranty boundaries

Great value is built when the customer understands everything included.

Approved Word Track - Three Parts

Part 1 - Demo the Problem

Remember the area we photographed outside? This is why that matters. The visible issue is only part of the story. The bigger issue is what is happening behind or around it if it is not corrected properly.

Part 2 - Demo the Product and Materials

Here is the material we use, here is how it is installed, and here is why it performs better than the lower-grade option. I want you to feel the difference, not just hear me talk about it.

Part 3 - Explain the Complete Project

The value is not just the product. It is the full system: removal, prep, repairs, proper installation, materials, skilled labor, cleanup, documentation, and written warranty terms. That is what you are investing in.

Required Customer Checkpoints

  • "Can you see why this is different from a patch-and-paint approach?"
  • "Does the complete project make sense based on what we saw on your home?"
  • "What part of this matters most to you: appearance, durability, lower maintenance, or protecting the home?"

Demo Quality Standard

A strong demo should:

  1. Tie back to the homeowner's actual problem.
  2. Use physical proof when available.
  3. Explain materials in plain language.
  4. Explain labor and installation requirements.
  5. Explain the full project scope.
  6. Connect features to homeowner benefits.
  7. Avoid unsupported technical claims.
  8. Confirm understanding before moving forward.

What Not To Say

Do not say or imply:

  • "This is maintenance-free."
  • "This will last forever."
  • "This is fireproof."
  • "This will lower your energy bill by ____."
  • "This is the best product on the market" unless supported by approved documentation.
  • "Everything is covered" unless the warranty file confirms exact coverage.

Product Fact Guardrail

Product facts must come from approved product files.

If a product fact is not confirmed, use:

I do not have that confirmed in the knowledge base. Do not quote it on a kitchen table. Flag it to your manager so we can get an approved answer.

KPI Impact

Step 5 affects:

  • Average Ticket
  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Close Rate
  • Price Resistance
  • Perceived Value

Internal training estimate: the physical demonstration should build roughly 25 percent to 30 percent of the customer's total perceived value before price.

Coaching Note

If the customer reacts strongly to price, review whether the rep explained the complete project during Step 5.

A weak demo makes the price feel like product cost instead of complete-project investment.
