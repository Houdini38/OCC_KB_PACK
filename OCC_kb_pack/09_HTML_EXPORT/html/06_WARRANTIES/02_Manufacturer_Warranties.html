Manufacturer Warranties by Product

Purpose

This document gives reps a simple product-by-product warranty reference.

Keep warranty explanations clear and honest. Do not overcomplicate unless the customer asks for details.

The signed contract and written warranty document are the final word.

Golden Rules

  1. Ask first: "Which product are we talking about?"
  2. SuperiorPRO generally covers installation / workmanship issues for 24 months on most jobs.
  3. Manufacturer coverage is separate and product-specific.
  4. SuperiorPRO helps with labor on manufacturer issues when the manufacturer approves and supplies covered materials.
  5. Never say "everything is covered."
  6. Never say "30-year workmanship."
  7. Never use "lifetime" unless the manufacturer specifically warrants that item for life.
  8. If unsure, use the safe default answer.

Safe Default Answer

SuperiorPRO warranties vary by product. We cover installation issues for 24 months on most jobs. Some products also have longer manufacturer coverage where we help with labor if the manufacturer approves. Your signed contract and warranty document are the final word.

Product Scripts

Hardie ColorPlus

For James Hardie ColorPlus, we cover our installation for 24 months. James Hardie gives a 30-year warranty on the product itself and 15 years on the factory ColorPlus finish. We will help with labor on any manufacturer issues only if Hardie approves and sends the material. Cleaning and caulking are not covered.

Primed Hardie + Paint

For primed James Hardie with paint finish, we cover our installation for 24 months. James Hardie backs the product itself with a 30-year substrate warranty and the paint finish has 5-year manufacturer coverage. We help with labor only if the manufacturer approves.

Painting

We give a solid 2-year workmanship warranty on painting. We fix our installation issues. We do not cover problems with the old surface underneath, knots bleeding, or areas that hold water.

Gutters

24 months on our installation. The 20-year part is for manufacturer defects, and we help with labor if they cover it. Normal wear, cleaning, and caulking are excluded.

Stucco

Straight 2-year workmanship warranty. Cracks, cleaning, and caulking maintenance are not covered.

Roofing

No matter which option you choose, our installation coverage is 24 months. The 20-year option gives us longer manufacturer support if they cover the defect. Ice damming is excluded.

Entry Doors

24 months on our installation work. Lifetime manufacturer help only where the door maker covers it. Normal wear and cleaning are excluded.

Common Warranty Questions

Customer: "What kind of warranty do I get?"

You get 24 months on our workmanship plus manufacturer coverage where it applies. I will show you the exact warranty page before you sign.

Customer: "Is everything covered?"

No warranty covers everything. We stand behind our installation and help with manufacturer issues we can. Things like storms, normal wear, cleaning, and caulking are not covered.

Customer: "So this is a 30-year warranty?" for Hardie

The Hardie product itself has a 30-year substrate warranty from James Hardie. Our workmanship is covered for 24 months. That is the honest way to explain it.

Customer: "What voids the warranty?"

Non-payment, letting another contractor work on it, or damage from storms, misuse, or harsh cleaners. Keep your paperwork and we will take care of you within the warranty terms.

Big Exclusions

Mention these when relevant:

  • Storms
  • Acts of God
  • Misuse
  • Alterations
  • Cleaning
  • Caulking
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Anything not on the original contract
  • Pre-existing structural issues
  • Third-party work after install

Hope Coaching Tips

  • Keep warranty coaching simple.
  • Do not interrupt for minor wording differences.
  • Correct immediately only when the rep overpromises.
  • Roleplay the two Hardie scripts so reps can explain ColorPlus vs Primed + Paint clearly.
  • Always emphasize: be clear and honest because it builds trust.

Final Guardrail

Never create new warranty terms.

If the exact contract or warranty document is not available, say so and direct the customer to SuperiorPRO for formal review.
