Company Workmanship Warranty

Purpose

This document explains SuperiorPRO's company workmanship warranty in simple rep-friendly language.

The goal is to help reps explain warranty clearly without overpromising or turning the conversation into legal language.

Simple Rule

SuperiorPRO generally covers installation / workmanship issues for 24 months on most projects.

Manufacturer coverage is separate.

The signed contract and written warranty document are the final word.

The Three-Layer Explanation

SuperiorPRO's warranty explanation should be framed as three layers:

  1. Manufacturer warranty: covers the product or material where applicable.
  2. SuperiorPRO workmanship warranty: covers installation and workmanship issues.
  3. SuperiorPRO labor help: supports manufacturer warranty issues when the manufacturer approves and supplies covered material.

Rep-Friendly Explanation

Use this simple explanation:

You get 24 months on our workmanship plus manufacturer coverage where it applies. I will show you the exact warranty page before you sign.

Safe Default Answer

Use this when unsure:

SuperiorPRO warranties vary by product. We cover installation issues for 24 months on most jobs. Some products also have longer manufacturer coverage where we help with labor if the manufacturer approves. Your signed contract and warranty document are the final word.

What Reps Should Make Clear

Reps should make clear that:

  • SuperiorPRO covers workmanship / installation issues for 24 months on most jobs.
  • Manufacturer warranty is separate from SuperiorPRO workmanship coverage.
  • Manufacturer coverage depends on the product and the manufacturer's approval.
  • SuperiorPRO helps with labor on manufacturer issues when manufacturer approval and materials apply.
  • The written warranty document and signed contract are the final word.

What Not To Say

Do not say:

  • "Everything is covered."
  • "You are covered forever."
  • "SuperiorPRO gives you a 30-year workmanship warranty."
  • "Labor is covered for the full manufacturer warranty period no matter what."
  • "The manufacturer will definitely approve it."
  • "This is lifetime" unless the specific manufacturer warranty says lifetime for that specific item.

Coaching Behavior

Hope should not interrupt for every small warranty wording issue.

Use soft coaching after the attempt unless the rep makes a clear overpromise.

Soft Correction Example

Good direction. Tighten the warranty explanation by separating our 24-month workmanship coverage from the manufacturer warranty. Say: "SuperiorPRO covers installation issues for 24 months, and the manufacturer may provide longer product coverage depending on the product and approval."

Hard Correction Example

Pause. That overpromises the warranty.
Issue: You said everything is covered.
Corrected language: "No warranty covers everything. We stand behind our installation and help with manufacturer issues we can. The written warranty document explains the exact terms."

KPI Impact

Clear warranty explanation supports:

  • Trust
  • Close Rate
  • Cancellation Reduction
  • Customer Experience
  • Fewer post-sale misunderstandings

Final Rule

Warranty should build trust through clarity.

When unsure, keep it simple and point to the written warranty document.
