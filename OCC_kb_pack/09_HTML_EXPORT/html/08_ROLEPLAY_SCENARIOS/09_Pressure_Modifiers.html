Pressure Modifiers

Purpose

Pressure Modifiers add realistic curveballs to roleplay without creating new personas.

Use pressure modifiers mostly in Medium and Hard roleplays.

Expert-style training should be handled as Hard Mode with one or more pressure modifiers.

Modifier Rules

  1. Modifiers must not override the selected persona's psychology.
  2. Modifiers must not create false facts.
  3. Modifiers must not require Hope or the rep to invent product, warranty, financing, pricing, or competitor claims.
  4. If a modifier introduces an unconfirmed fact request, score the rep on whether they safely verify instead of guessing.
  5. Use one modifier for Medium roleplays.
  6. Use one to three modifiers for Hard roleplays.
  7. Do not overload new reps with compound modifiers.

Pressure Modifier Menu

PM-01 - Early Price Demand

Customer says early:

Can you just give me the price?

Tests:

  • Step 1 expectation control
  • Process discipline
  • Appointment flow

Rep must:

  • Acknowledge
  • Promise exact price later
  • Explain why inspection and scope come first
  • Hold the process

PM-02 - Competitor Quote Appears

Customer reveals a lower quote mid-appointment or during Step 8.

Tests:

  • Step 3 differentiation
  • Step 4 value frame
  • Apples-to-apples comparison
  • Margin discipline

Rep must:

  • Ask to compare scope
  • Avoid competitor attacks
  • Avoid early discounting

PM-03 - Spouse Leaves Room

One decision-maker disengages or leaves.

Tests:

  • Decision-maker control
  • Process control
  • Emotional awareness

Rep must:

  • Pause respectfully
  • Bring the decision-maker back if possible
  • Surface what is not sitting right
  • Avoid selling to only one person if both are needed

PM-04 - Adult Child Calls In

Adult child joins by phone and warns the parent not to sign.

Tests:

  • Trust
  • Family influence
  • Calm control
  • Clear explanation

Rep must:

  • Greet the child respectfully
  • Address concerns openly
  • Offer written documentation
  • Avoid pushing past family concern

PM-05 - Customer Fact-Checks Live

Customer searches product, warranty, financing, or company claims during roleplay.

Tests:

  • Approved facts
  • Calm authority
  • Missing-information response
  • Compliance

Rep must:

  • Use approved facts only
  • Admit when something needs verification
  • Avoid bluffing

PM-06 - Compound Objections

Customer stacks multiple objections at once.

Example:

Your price is high, I have another quote, and I need to think about it.

Tests:

  • Objection isolation
  • Composure
  • Listening

Rep must:

  • Not answer all three at once
  • Isolate the most important concern
  • Ask which issue matters most

PM-07 - Attorney Mention

Customer says:

I had an attorney look at the last contract, and I am being careful.

Tests:

  • Composure
  • Documentation confidence
  • No defensiveness

Rep must:

  • Welcome review
  • Stay calm
  • Avoid legal advice
  • Offer to clarify contract items through proper channels

PM-08 - Impossible Timeline

Customer wants a timeline that may not be possible.

Tests:

  • Operational honesty
  • Trust
  • No overpromising

Rep must:

  • Avoid guaranteeing dates
  • Explain production will confirm schedule
  • Offer to flag urgency internally without promising outcome

PM-09 - Unconfirmed Product Spec Request

Customer asks for a specific R-value, U-factor, wind rating, fire rating, or technical spec not confirmed in the KB.

Tests:

  • Product compliance
  • Honesty
  • Knowledge boundaries

Rep must:

  • Not guess
  • Use missing-information response
  • Offer to verify

PM-10 - ROI / Resale Claim Request

Customer asks:

How much value will this add to my home?

Tests:

  • ROI claim discipline
  • Value framing
  • No financial guarantees

Rep must:

  • Avoid guaranteed ROI
  • Discuss value, curb appeal, maintenance burden, and marketability
  • Verify any specific numbers before quoting

PM-11 - Live Camera / Public Content

Customer records, livestreams, or asks the rep to speak on camera.

Tests:

  • Composure
  • Clean language
  • Compliance
  • Brand professionalism

Rep must:

  • Stay approved and professional
  • Avoid speculative claims
  • Avoid casual discount talk
  • Treat every word as public

PM-12 - Hostile Interruption

Customer interrupts emotionally or aggressively.

Tests:

  • Emotional control
  • Listening
  • Trust rebuilding

Rep must:

  • Let the customer vent briefly
  • Acknowledge
  • Redirect to proof and process
  • Avoid defensive tone

Modifier Selection Guide

ROI discipline PM-10 ROI / Resale Claim Request
Public composure PM-11 Live Camera / Public Content
Emotional control PM-12 Hostile Interruption

Scoring Guidance

When a pressure modifier is used, Hope should score:

  • Did the rep stay composed?
  • Did the rep stay inside the sales system?
  • Did the rep isolate the real concern?
  • Did the rep avoid unsupported claims?
  • Did the rep protect margin and process control?
  • Did the rep return to the correct step?

Final Rule

Pressure modifiers should make the roleplay harder, not messier.

Use pressure to expose weak process, not to create chaos.
