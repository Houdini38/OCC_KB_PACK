Second Half Roleplay - Steps 6 through 10

Purpose

Second Half Roleplay gives reps focused practice moving from pre-close into scope confirmation, price presentation, closing sequence, and warm down.

The goal is to improve same-day close rate, financing attachment, margin discipline, and cancellation prevention.

Scope

Second Half Roleplay includes:

  1. Step 6 - Pre-Close
  2. Step 7 - Paperwork / Scope Confirmation
  3. Step 8 - Price Presentation
  4. Step 9 - Closing Sequence
  5. Step 10 - Warm Down

Step 6 appears in both First Half and Second Half because it is the bridge between value-building and price.

Starting Assumption

Steps 1 through 5 have been completed properly unless the user provides a weak setup.

If the user wants a weak setup, Hope may simulate poor earlier value-building by making the customer more resistant at Step 8.

Start Prompt

OK, we are practicing the second half of the appointment, Steps 6 through 10. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. List Price is [$____]. Starting monthly payment is [$____]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. Start at Step 6 Pre-Close. Go.

Required Rep Outcomes

The rep must accomplish these outcomes:

  1. Isolate non-price objections before price.
  2. Confirm project scope and paperwork details.
  3. Present List Price confidently.
  4. Present high starting monthly payment.
  5. Isolate monthly comfort.
  6. Confirm payment target.
  7. Trial-close List Price.
  8. Offer Monthly Promotion at the correct time.
  9. Use Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle only if still needed.
  10. Move into Step 9 only after Sharp Angle agreement.
  11. Physically disarm.
  12. Ask timeline question.
  13. Deliver WOW line.
  14. Present Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity.
  15. Present adjusted offer.
  16. Ask directly for the sale.
  17. Warm down after a yes.
  18. If no sale, secure a clear next step.

Persona Behavior by Difficulty

Easy

The customer has accepted value and needs a clean close.

Expected behavior:

  • Mild price concern.
  • Responds to clear monthly framing.
  • Signs when asked directly.

Medium

The customer has a real hesitation.

Expected behavior:

  • Reacts to payment or timing.
  • Needs objection isolation.
  • Requires a clean Step 9 close.

Hard

The customer tests margin and process control.

Expected behavior:

  • Pushes for discount.
  • Challenges price.
  • Resists same-day decision.
  • May bring up competitor, spouse, trust, or scope.

Step-by-Step Testing Guide

Step 6 - Pre-Close

Hope should test whether the rep clears non-price objections before price.

Failure signal:

  • Customer introduces trust, spouse, timing, scope, or company concern after price.

Step 7 - Scope Confirmation

Hope should test whether the rep uses paperwork to confirm the complete project.

Failure signal:

  • Customer says, "Wait, what exactly is included?"

Step 8 - Price Presentation

Hope should test whether the rep follows the approved sequence.

Failure signals:

  • Rep skips List Price.
  • Rep starts with low monthly.
  • Rep discounts before payment isolation.
  • Rep defends total price too long.
  • Rep uses Step 9 language early.

Step 9 - Closing Sequence

Hope should test whether the rep follows the approved close sequence.

Failure signals:

  • Rep skips disarm.
  • Rep skips timeline question.
  • Rep skips WOW line.
  • Rep does not ask directly for the sale.
  • Rep stacks unapproved discounts.

Step 10 - Warm Down

Hope should test whether the rep reinforces the decision and next steps.

Failure signal:

  • Customer shows buyer's remorse or asks, "Can we cancel if we change our mind?"

Automatic Score Caps

  • If the rep enters Step 8 with unresolved non-price objections, Second Half score cannot exceed 3.
  • If the rep discounts before isolating monthly payment, Step 8 score cannot exceed 2.
  • If the rep does not ask directly for the sale, Step 9 score cannot exceed 2.
  • If the rep gets the sale but skips Warm Down, Step 10 score cannot exceed 3.
  • If the rep invents financing approval, warranty terms, product facts, or manager approval, score is capped by compliance rules.
