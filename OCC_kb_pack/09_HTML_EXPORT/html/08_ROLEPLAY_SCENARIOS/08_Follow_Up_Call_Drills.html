Follow Up Call Drills

Purpose

Follow Up Call Drills help reps practice calling homeowners who did not buy during the original appointment.

This is not a script test.

Reps may use their own language as long as they accomplish the required outcomes.

Required Follow-Up Outcomes

A strong follow-up call should accomplish these outcomes:

  1. Reopen professionally.
  2. Reference the original appointment.
  3. Confirm the homeowner's current status.
  4. Isolate what is still holding them back.
  5. Reconnect to the original problem and urgency.
  6. Reconfirm value and scope without restarting the entire presentation.
  7. Address the real remaining objection.
  8. Avoid panic discounting.
  9. Ask for a clear decision or scheduled next action.
  10. Log the true objection and next step.

Start Prompt

OK, Follow Up Call Mode. I will be [Persona] receiving your call after a no-sale appointment. Original project was [Product]. The unresolved issue was [Objection]. Your goal is [decision / scheduled revisit / clear next step]. Start the call when ready.

Follow-Up Drill Types

FU-01 - Think About It Callback

Customer left saying:

We need to think about it.

Best personas:

  • Hesitant Helen
  • Skeptical Steve
  • Retired Ruth

Rep goal:

  • Reopen professionally.
  • Ask what specifically they have been thinking about.
  • Isolate project, company, timing, or investment.
  • Ask for a decision or scheduled revisit.

KPI focus:

  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Same-Week Recovery
  • Objection Isolation

FU-02 - Other Estimates Callback

Customer left saying:

We want to get another estimate.

Best personas:

  • Comparison Carl
  • Skeptical Steve
  • Bargain Betty

Rep goal:

  • Ask what they learned from the other estimate.
  • Compare scope without attacking.
  • Isolate whether price, scope, company, or timing is the issue.
  • Ask for next step.

KPI focus:

  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Shopping Resistance
  • Margin

FU-03 - Spouse / Decision-Maker Callback

Customer left saying:

I need to talk to my spouse.

Best personas:

  • Hesitant Helen
  • Budget Brenda
  • Retired Ruth

Rep goal:

  • Ask how the spouse conversation went.
  • Identify what the spouse cared about most.
  • Offer to schedule a short revisit with both decision-makers.
  • Avoid forcing the present homeowner to sell the job alone.

KPI focus:

  • Decision-Maker Control
  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Follow-Up Quality

FU-04 - Price Too High Callback

Customer left saying:

It was too expensive.

Best personas:

  • Budget Brenda
  • Bargain Betty
  • Comparison Carl

Rep goal:

  • Reopen without apologizing for price.
  • Ask whether the concern is total investment or monthly payment.
  • Reconnect to the original problem.
  • Avoid panic discounting.

KPI focus:

  • Margin
  • Financing Attachment
  • Rehash Close Rate

FU-05 - Timing / Not Ready Callback

Customer left saying:

We are not ready yet.

Best personas:

  • Hesitant Helen
  • Retired Ruth
  • Skeptical Steve

Rep goal:

  • Ask what has changed since the appointment.
  • Reconnect to the original issue and consequence.
  • Define what would make them ready.
  • Schedule a specific next step.

KPI focus:

  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Same-Week Recovery
  • CRM Follow-Up Quality

FU-06 - Trust Concern Callback

Customer left uncertain because of trust, contractor concern, or prior bad experience.

Best personas:

  • Angry Arnold
  • Skeptical Steve
  • Retired Ruth

Rep goal:

  • Acknowledge the concern without defensiveness.
  • Ask what trust issue remains unresolved.
  • Offer written scope, documentation, or manager follow-up where appropriate.
  • Ask for a clear next step.

KPI focus:

  • Trust
  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Cancellation Prevention

What Not To Do

Do not:

  • Say "just checking in" with no purpose.
  • Apologize for the original price.
  • Immediately offer a new discount.
  • Invent a new promotion.
  • Promise financing approval.
  • Trash competitors.
  • Restart the entire presentation unless the customer asks.
  • Pressure the customer after a clear decline.
  • Use Step 9 Promo Home language as a fresh callback offer unless approved by manager policy.

Final Rule

A follow-up call is not a casual check-in.

It is a professional recovery attempt with a clear purpose, clear objection isolation, and a clear next action.
