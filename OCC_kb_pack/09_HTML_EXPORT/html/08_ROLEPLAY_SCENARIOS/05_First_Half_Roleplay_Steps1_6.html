First Half Roleplay - Steps 1 through 6

Purpose

First Half Roleplay gives reps focused practice on the value-building half of the SuperiorPRO 10-step sales system.

The goal is to improve the rep's ability to earn trust, create urgency, differentiate SuperiorPRO, condition price, demonstrate value, and isolate non-price objections before price.

Scope

First Half Roleplay includes:

  1. Step 1 - Warm-Up
  2. Step 2 - Pre-Assessment / Inspection
  3. Step 3 - Company Story / Challenger Method
  4. Step 4 - Investment / Price Conditioning
  5. Step 5 - Product Demonstration
  6. Step 6 - Pre-Close

The roleplay ends after Step 6 when the rep has isolated that investment is the only remaining issue.

Start Prompt

OK, we are practicing the first half of the appointment, Steps 1 through 6. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. Your goal is to build value and isolate that investment is the only thing left before price. You are at the door. Go.

Required Rep Outcomes

The rep must accomplish these outcomes:

  1. Set appointment expectations.
  2. Confirm appointment length.
  3. Identify decision-maker involvement.
  4. Involve the homeowner in the inspection.
  5. Create urgency using the actual home.
  6. Teach the Three Ways to Do Business.
  7. Condition price before price.
  8. Demonstrate product and complete-project value.
  9. Ask the Step 6 pre-close question.
  10. Isolate whether anything besides investment would prevent the sale.

Persona Behavior by Difficulty

Easy

The customer cooperates but still needs clear leadership.

Expected behavior:

  • Gives buying signals.
  • Asks basic questions.
  • Accepts the appointment flow if explained clearly.
  • Needs mild guidance to stay focused.

Medium

The customer creates realistic resistance.

Expected behavior:

  • Asks for price early.
  • Downplays urgency.
  • Brings up timing or spouse.
  • Needs objection isolation.

Hard

The customer challenges the rep's process.

Expected behavior:

  • Pushes for price before value.
  • Questions company differentiation.
  • Challenges product claims.
  • Creates trust, timing, or comparison pressure.

Step-by-Step Testing Guide

Step 1 - Warm-Up

Hope should test whether the rep:

  • Explains what will happen.
  • Confirms appointment length.
  • Confirms decision-maker involvement.
  • Holds the process when customer asks for quick price.

Failure signal:

  • Customer later rushes the rep or says, "I thought this was just an estimate."

Step 2 - Inspection

Hope should test whether the rep:

  • Gets the homeowner involved.
  • Points out specific issues.
  • Explains cause and consequence.
  • Uses Past-Future-Present.

Failure signal:

  • Customer says, "It does not seem that bad," or "We can probably wait."

Step 3 - Company Story

Hope should test whether the rep:

  • Teaches DIY vs contractor vs SuperiorPRO.
  • Tailors the explanation to the home.
  • Differentiates without attacking.

Failure signal:

  • Customer says, "We may just get a local guy to do it."

Step 4 - Price Conditioning

Hope should test whether the rep:

  • Uses investment language.
  • Frames cost versus value.
  • Avoids actual pricing.
  • Transitions to demo.

Failure signal:

  • Customer says, "Just tell me if this is going to be expensive."

Step 5 - Product Demonstration

Hope should test whether the rep:

  • Makes value physical.
  • Explains materials and labor.
  • Explains complete project scope.
  • Avoids unsupported claims.

Failure signal:

  • Customer says, "I still do not see why this is different."

Step 6 - Pre-Close

Hope should test whether the rep:

  • Confirms custom-order logic.
  • Confirms when-not-if.
  • Asks if anything besides investment would prevent moving forward.

Failure signal:

  • Customer gives vague delay language or introduces a non-price concern after price.

Automatic Score Caps

  • If the rep jumps to price before Step 6, First Half score cannot exceed 2.
  • If the rep fails to involve the homeowner in the inspection, First Half score cannot exceed 3.
  • If the rep never creates urgency, First Half score cannot exceed 3.
  • If the rep does not isolate non-price objections in Step 6, First Half score cannot exceed 3.
  • If the rep invents product, warranty, financing, or competitor facts, score is capped by compliance rules.

The first half earns the right to present price.

If value is weak before Step 8, the close becomes a discount fight.
