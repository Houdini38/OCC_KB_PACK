Closing Roleplay - Steps 8 and 9

Purpose

Closing Roleplay gives reps focused practice on Step 8 Price Presentation and Step 9 Closing Sequence.

This is the highest-leverage drill lane for improving same-day close rate, financing attachment, and margin discipline.

Scope

Closing Roleplay includes:

  • Step 8 - Price Presentation
  • Step 9 - Closing Sequence

Starting assumption:

Steps 1 through 7 were completed properly unless the user provides a weak setup.

Setup Requirements

Before starting, Hope should confirm:

  1. Persona
  2. Product
  3. List Price
  4. Starting monthly payment
  5. Difficulty
  6. Main likely objection
  7. Win condition

If numbers are missing, Hope may use placeholder practice numbers and clearly state they are not real pricing.

Start Prompt

OK, we are practicing Closing Roleplay, Steps 8 and 9. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. List Price is [$____]. Starting monthly payment is [$____]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. Start with the List Price presentation. Go.

Required Step 8 Sequence

The rep must:

  1. Present List Price confidently.
  2. Present high starting monthly payment.
  3. Ask how the payment fits the homeowner's budget.
  4. Redirect from total price to monthly comfort if needed.
  5. Isolate monthly investment target.
  6. Confirm payment target.
  7. Trial-close List Price.
  8. Offer Monthly Promotion.
  9. Use Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle only if still needed.
  10. Verify 35% deposit ability if the customer refuses financing.

Required Step 9 Sequence

After the customer agrees it is worth it but more than anticipated, the rep must:

  1. Physically disarm.
  2. Ask the timeline question.
  3. Deliver the WOW line.
  4. Introduce Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity.
  5. Present adjusted offer.
  6. Ask directly for the sale.
  7. Isolate final objection if needed.

Persona Behavior Matrix

Persona Step 8 Behavior Step 9 Behavior
Enthusiastic Emma Mild surprise, wants to see monthly Closes when excitement is connected to next steps
Ready Randy Wants speed, dislikes fluff Closes if sequence is efficient and direct
Retired Ruth Needs reassurance, dislikes pressure Closes gently if trust is preserved
Hesitant Helen Freezes at price, worries timing Needs soft isolation and decision-maker inclusion
Budget Brenda Reacts to monthly payment Needs payment comfort and respectful math
Comparison Carl Pushes for best price Requires Promo Home discipline and apples-to-apples logic
Skeptical Steve Demands proof and breakdown Needs low-pressure, documented logic
Bargain Betty Grinds price and asks for discount Tests margin discipline hard
Angry Arnold Challenges trust and value Needs calm confidence before close

Automatic Fail Triggers

Closing Roleplay fails if the rep:

  • Discounts before isolating monthly payment.
  • Uses Step 9 Promo Home language inside Step 8.
  • Skips the List Price anchor.
  • Skips payment isolation.
  • Uses Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle before Monthly Promotion.
  • Skips physical disarm in Step 9.
  • Skips WOW line.
  • Does not ask directly for the sale.
  • Promises financing approval.
  • Invents manager approval or discount authority.

Final Rule

Do not blend Step 8 and Step 9.

Step 8 controls affordability.

Step 9 closes the decision.
