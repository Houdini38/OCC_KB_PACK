Objection Drill Bank

Purpose

Objection Drill Bank gives reps short, focused practice handling one objection at a time.

The goal is to build automatic objection isolation without turning reps into script robots.

Drill Format

Hope opens with one objection in the selected persona's voice.

The rep must respond using the approved objection framework:

Acknowledge
Isolate
Clarify
Respond
Tie Back
Close Back

Start Prompt

OK, objection drill. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. I will give you one objection. Your job is to acknowledge, isolate, clarify, respond, tie back, and close back. Ready?

Universal Scoring Criteria

Hope should score whether the rep:

  1. Acknowledged without arguing.
  2. Isolated the real objection.
  3. Clarified in the customer's words.
  4. Responded using approved system language.
  5. Tied back to an earlier agreement or home-specific issue.
  6. Closed back with a question.
  7. Avoided unsupported facts, early discounts, or pressure.

Core Objection Drills

OD-01 - Price Too High

Customer says:

That is too much.

Best personas:

  • Budget Brenda
  • Comparison Carl
  • Bargain Betty
  • Skeptical Steve

Rep goal:

  • Stay inside Step 8.
  • Redirect from total price to monthly comfort.
  • Do not discount before isolating payment.

KPI focus:

  • Margin
  • Financing Attachment
  • Close Rate

OD-02 - Need To Think About It

Customer says:

I need to think about it.

Best personas:

  • Hesitant Helen
  • Skeptical Steve
  • Retired Ruth

Rep goal:

  • Isolate what specifically needs thought: project, company, timing, or investment.
  • Do not accept vague delay.

KPI focus:

  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Objection Isolation
  • Rehash Close Rate

OD-03 - Need To Talk To Spouse

Customer says:

I need to talk to my spouse.

Best personas:

  • Hesitant Helen
  • Retired Ruth
  • Budget Brenda

Rep goal:

  • Identify whether this is a real decision-maker issue or a stall.
  • If spouse is present, engage them directly.
  • If spouse is absent, create a specific next step.

KPI focus:

  • Decision-Maker Control
  • Same-Day Close Rate

OD-04 - Getting Other Estimates

Customer says:

We want to get other estimates.

Best personas:

  • Comparison Carl
  • Skeptical Steve
  • Bargain Betty

Rep goal:

  • Reframe to apples-to-apples comparison.
  • Compare scope, prep, materials, installation, cleanup, and written warranty documents.
  • Do not attack competitors.

KPI focus:

  • Shopping Resistance
  • Close Rate
  • Margin

OD-05 - Not Ready

Customer says:

We are not ready yet.

Best personas:

  • Hesitant Helen
  • Retired Ruth
  • Skeptical Steve

Rep goal:

  • Isolate timing vs project vs company vs investment.
  • Reconnect to Step 2 urgency.

KPI focus:

  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Urgency
  • Rehash Close Rate

OD-06 - No Time Today

Customer says:

I thought this would be quick. Can you just give me the number?

Best personas:

  • Ready Randy
  • Comparison Carl
  • Skeptical Steve

Rep goal:

  • Reset appointment expectations.
  • Hold process without sounding defensive.
  • Offer continue or reschedule if needed.

KPI focus:

  • Process Compliance
  • Appointment Completion
  • Close Rate

OD-07 - Do Not Want Financing

Customer says:

We do not finance things. We pay cash.

Best personas:

  • Budget Brenda
  • Ready Randy
  • Retired Ruth

Rep goal:

  • Respect cash preference.
  • Verify ability to pay 35% down and balance upon completion.
  • Avoid financing pressure and prohibited claims.

KPI focus:

  • Financing Attachment
  • Margin
  • Close Rate

OD-08 - Trust Concern

Customer says:

How do I know I can trust you?

Best personas:

  • Angry Arnold
  • Skeptical Steve
  • Retired Ruth

Rep goal:

  • Acknowledge concern.
  • Use process, documentation, and scope clarity.
  • Do not say "trust me."

KPI focus:

  • Trust
  • Close Rate
  • Cancellation Reduction

OD-09 - Same-Day Close Resistance

Customer says:

We do not make decisions the same day.

Best personas:

  • Skeptical Steve
  • Retired Ruth
  • Hesitant Helen

Rep goal:

  • Avoid pressure.
  • Isolate the real concern.
  • Ask what must be verified before moving forward.

KPI focus:

  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Margin

OD-10 - Partial Scope Request

Customer says:

Can we just do part of it for now?

Best personas:

  • Budget Brenda
  • Bargain Betty
  • Hesitant Helen

Rep goal:

  • Determine whether partial scope is valid or risky.
  • Reconnect to complete project value.
  • Do not agree to scope that creates project risk.

KPI focus:

  • Average Ticket
  • Scope Control
  • Close Rate

OD-11 - Contractor Family Member

Customer says:

My brother-in-law is a contractor. I want him to look at this.

Best personas:

  • Skeptical Steve
  • Comparison Carl
  • Angry Arnold

Rep goal:

  • Respect the family member.
  • Ask whether they specialize in the specific product.
  • Compare scope and installation requirements without trash-talk.

KPI focus:

  • Shopping Resistance
  • Trust
  • Close Rate

OD-12 - Warranty Question

Customer says:

What kind of warranty do I get?

Best personas:

  • Retired Ruth
  • Skeptical Steve
  • Ready Randy

Rep goal:

  • Give simple warranty explanation.
  • Separate SuperiorPRO workmanship from manufacturer coverage.
  • Point to written warranty.
  • Do not overpromise.

KPI focus:

  • Trust
  • Cancellation Reduction
  • Close Rate

Persona Voice Guidance

Use the objection in the persona's style.

Examples:

  • Budget Brenda: worried and financial.
  • Comparison Carl: sharp and negotiation-driven.
  • Bargain Betty: persistent and discount-focused.
  • Skeptical Steve: proof-driven and suspicious.
  • Angry Arnold: blunt and distrustful.
  • Retired Ruth: polite and cautious.

Final Rule

Do not let the rep answer the surface objection too fast.

The best reps isolate before they overcome.
