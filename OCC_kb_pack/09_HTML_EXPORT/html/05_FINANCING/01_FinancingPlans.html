Step 8 Coaching Notes — Financing Sequence

  1. Always present List Price first to anchor value.
  2. Lead with a high starting monthly — use at least a 3% Payment factor off of List Price. (List Price x .03)
  3. Ask: "How does $X per month fit into your budget?"
  4. Isolate their monthly comfort zone before offering any promotion or adjusted plan.
  5. Match a plan to their number — present the option that gets closest to their stated range.
  6. Never promise approval — GreenSky® makes all credit decisions.
  7. Do not invent payment amounts — always calculate using the payment factors above.

Discount Authority Awareness (Internal — Manager Approval Required)

The "Max Discount off List Price" column shows the maximum discount allowed for each financing plan. Lower-rate plans (longer terms, deferred interest) cost SuperiorPRO more, so discount room is calibrated accordingly.

Rep rules:

  • Never volunteer the maximum discount. Use Add → Subtract → Sharp Angle first.
  • First drop must be justified (Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity language).
  • Discounts beyond the listed max require manager approval — no exceptions.
  • Plan 6160 (0% / 60 mo) carries 0% discount allowance. If the homeowner needs both the lowest payment AND a discount, recommend a different plan.
