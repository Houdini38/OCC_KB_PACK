Financing Overview

SuperiorPRO offers financing as a buying tool, not as a discount substitute. The right financing presentation eliminates the #1 objection: "I can't afford this."

Approved Financing Plans

SuperiorPRO offers 13 active GreenSky® plans across 4 categories. Full plan numbers, rates, terms, payment factors, and max discount allowances are documented in 40_FINANCING/01_FinancingPlans.md.

Universal Financing Rules

  • Use only approved plans (no promising terms outside what the lender controls)
  • Always pair the pitch with the required disclosures
  • Never promise approval, rate, or term outside what the lender controls
  • Always run the credit application at the kitchen table — soft pull first
  • Set the expectation that the lender's decision is the lender's, not ours

Discount Authority Rules

  • Never volunteer the maximum discount. Use Add → Subtract → Sharp Angle first.
  • First drop must be justified (Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity language).
  • Discounts beyond the listed max require manager approval — no exceptions.
  • Plan 6160 carries 0% discount allowance. If the homeowner needs both the lowest 0% APR payment AND a discount, recommend a different plan.
  • Discount maximums vary by plan — see 01_FinancingPlans.md for the full table.

When to Lead With Financing

  • Always mention financing exists during Step 8 (Price Presentation)
  • Lead with monthly payment after presenting List Price
  • Use financing as the primary affordability lever, not discount
  • Per the Step 8 mastery doc: do not move forward until the customer has given a monthly number OR clearly stated they will not finance

When NOT to Push Financing

  • When the customer is a clear cash buyer and confirms it twice
  • When a hard credit pull would be inappropriate or unwanted
  • When there's a clear scope/value problem — fix that first, financing won't save a weak presentation

Hope's Coaching Triggers

Hope flags these errors during financing presentation:

  • Rep mentions financing only after the customer objects to total
  • Rep skips the disclosure language
  • Rep promises a specific rate or term
  • Rep says "free financing" or "guaranteed approval"
  • Rep doesn't isolate the customer's monthly comfort zone before presenting the promotion offer
  • Rep volunteers a discount before isolating the customer's monthly comfort zone
  • Rep offers a discount above the plan's maximum discount allowance without manager approval
  • Rep recommends Plan 6160 alongside a discount request (incompatible)
