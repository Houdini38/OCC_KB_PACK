Sales Process Checklist

Purpose

This checklist is the fast diagnostic version of the Sales System Master.

Use it to determine whether a rep followed the required sales process and where the breakdown occurred.

This checklist does not replace the Sales System Master. If this checklist conflicts with the Sales System Master, the Sales System Master wins.

Core Rule

A missed step creates a future objection.

The agent should use this checklist to identify:

  • Which step was followed
  • Which step was skipped
  • Which checkpoint was missed
  • Which KPI was affected
  • What the rep should practice next

Step 1 - Warm-Up Checklist

Required Actions

  • Greet professionally.
  • Confirm the homeowner understands the appointment agenda.
  • Explain inspection, measurement, sit-down review, specifications, and pricing.
  • Ask: "Fair enough?"
  • Ask whether the office explained the appointment length.
  • Explain the appointment typically takes about an hour and a half depending on questions.
  • Confirm the time still works.
  • Confirm who will be involved in the decision.

Pass / Fail

Pass if the homeowner agrees to the appointment flow and timing.

Fail if the rep rushes into inspection, product, or price without setting expectations.

KPI Impact

Process Compliance and Close Rate.

Step 2 - Pre-Assessment / Inspection Checklist

Required Actions

  • Walk the home with the homeowner whenever possible.
  • Take wide-angle and close-up photos.
  • Point out specific issues on the home.
  • Explain cause, consequence, and timing.
  • Use Past-Future-Present urgency.
  • Get the homeowner to acknowledge the problem.

Pass / Fail

Pass if the homeowner agrees the issue is real and should be addressed.

Fail if the rep inspects silently or does not create urgency.

KPI Impact

Same-Day Close Rate and Close Rate.

Step 3 - Company Story / Challenger Method Checklist

Required Actions

  • Teach the Three Ways to Do Business.
  • Explain DIY risk.
  • Explain contractor / partial-fix risk.
  • Explain SuperiorPRO's complete-scope approach.
  • Tailor the explanation to the actual home inspection.
  • Confirm the customer wants the job done once and done right.

Pass / Fail

Pass if the homeowner understands why the decision is not just lowest price.

Fail if the rep gives a generic company pitch or competitor-bashes.

KPI Impact

Close Rate and Shopping Resistance.

Step 4 - Investment / Price Conditioning Checklist

Required Actions

  • Use investment language before price.
  • Frame the project as cost-vs-value.
  • Explain cheap vs average vs long-term value.
  • Discuss lifetime cost, maintenance, durability, and peace of mind.
  • Do not reveal actual price or monthly payment.
  • Transition into product demonstration.

Pass / Fail

Pass if the homeowner agrees that quality and long-term value matter before price is shown.

Fail if the rep skips price conditioning or reveals price too early.

KPI Impact

Average Ticket and Close Rate.

Step 5 - Product Demonstration Checklist

Required Actions

  • Demonstrate the product physically.
  • Connect demo points to the homeowner's actual problem.
  • Explain materials used.
  • Explain labor involved.
  • Explain prep, repairs, installation, cleanup, and warranty boundaries.
  • Confirm the homeowner understands the complete project.

Pass / Fail

Pass if the homeowner can explain why the complete project is different from a patch or cheap option.

Fail if the rep only shows samples without explaining complete project value.

KPI Impact

Average Ticket and Same-Day Close.

Step 6 - Pre-Close Checklist

Required Actions

  • Explain custom-order logic.
  • Confirm the project is a when decision, not an if decision.
  • Ask whether anything other than investment would prevent the homeowner from doing business.
  • Stop and resolve any non-price objection.

Pass / Fail

Pass if price is isolated as the only remaining objection.

Fail if the rep goes to price while trust, product, scope, timing, or decision-maker concerns remain unresolved.

KPI Impact

Same-Day Close Rate and Objection Isolation.

Step 7 - Paperwork / Scope Confirmation Checklist

Required Actions

  • Complete spec sheets accurately.
  • Explain all major scope items.
  • Confirm materials, labor, options, colors, and exclusions.
  • Ask whether anything is missing.
  • Confirm this is the project the homeowner wants if the investment makes sense.

Pass / Fail

Pass if scope is accurate and confirmed before price.

Fail if paperwork is treated as admin instead of scope control.

KPI Impact

Production Accuracy, Cancellation Reduction, and Process Compliance.

Step 8 - Price Presentation Checklist

Required Actions

The rep must follow the approved Step 8 sequence:

  1. Present List Price confidently.
  2. Present a high starting monthly payment.
  3. Ask how that payment fits the homeowner's budget.
  4. Redirect from total price to monthly comfort if needed.
  5. Isolate the monthly investment target.
  6. Confirm the payment target.
  7. Trial-close List Price.
  8. Offer the Monthly Promotion.
  9. Use Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle only if still needed.
  10. If the customer refuses financing, verify ability to pay the 35% deposit and move forward.

Required Questions

  • "How does $____ per month fit into your budget?"
  • "What monthly investment would feel comfortable for you?"
  • "One being the worst and Ten the best, where would you put your credit?"

Pass / Fail

Pass if the rep isolates payment before promotion and does not panic-discount.

Fail if the rep offers promotion before the customer gives a payment target or clearly refuses financing.

KPI Impact

Financing Attachment, Margin, Close Rate, and Same-Day Close.

Drift Watch

Do not blend Step 8 with Step 9 Promo Home language.

Step 9 - Closing Sequence Checklist

Required Actions

The rep must follow the approved Step 9 sequence:

  1. Begin after the Sharp Angle agreement.
  2. Physically disarm.
  3. Ask the timeline question.
  4. Deliver the WOW line.
  5. Present the justified Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity language.
  6. Present the adjusted offer.
  7. Ask directly for the sale.
  8. Isolate the final objection if the customer hesitates.

Required Moves

  • Physical disarm before the timeline question.
  • Required line: "Wow, you must be pretty motivated to get this project done."
  • Required ask: "Based on this adjusted offer, are you comfortable moving forward with the project today?"

Pass / Fail

Pass if the rep follows the approved close sequence and asks directly for the sale.

Fail if the rep blends Step 8 language into Step 9, skips the disarm, or throws unapproved discounts at the objection.

KPI Impact

Same-Day Close Rate and Margin.

Drift Watch

Do not place Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle inside Step 9. It belongs in Step 8 before Step 9 begins.

Step 10 - Warm Down Checklist

Required Actions

  • Slow down after the customer says yes.
  • Reinforce the decision.
  • Confirm project next steps.
  • Confirm contact information.
  • Clarify what happens next.
  • Prevent buyer's remorse.
  • Ask for review or referral at the appropriate time.

Pass / Fail

Pass if the customer feels calm, clear, and confident after the decision.

Fail if the rep rushes out immediately after the signature.

KPI Impact

Cancellation Reduction, Reviews, Referrals, and Customer Experience.

Follow Up Call Mode Checklist

Follow Up Call Mode is not a script test. Reps can use their own words.

Required Outcomes

A follow-up call should accomplish these outcomes:

  1. Reopen professionally.
  2. Reference the original appointment.
  3. Isolate what is still holding the customer back.
  4. Reconnect to the original problem.
  5. Avoid restarting the whole presentation.
  6. Avoid panic discounting.
  7. Ask for a decision or a scheduled next action.

Pass / Fail

Pass if the rep earns either a clear decision or a scheduled next action.

Fail if the rep says "just checking in," avoids the real objection, or immediately discounts.

KPI Impact

Rehash Close Rate, Overall Close Rate, Same-Week Recovery Rate, and CRM Follow-Up Quality.
