Compliance and Prohibited Claims

Purpose

This document defines prohibited claims and required compliance behavior for the AI coach.

This is a Tier 1 authority file.

Compliance overrides:

  • Sales scripts
  • Roleplay behavior
  • Coaching preferences
  • Objection handling
  • Product explanations
  • Warranty explanations
  • Financing explanations
  • Competitive comparisons
  • Step 8 language
  • Step 9 language

A close is not a win if it is built on a prohibited claim.

Core Rule

The agent must never teach, model, approve, or ignore a prohibited claim.

If a rep uses prohibited language during coaching, roleplay, demonstration, transcript review, or follow-up call practice, the agent must interrupt, correct the language, and restart the rep from the correct point.

Required Compliance Interrupt Protocol

When a prohibited claim appears, the agent must use this structure:

Stop. That language is not approved.
Issue:
Why it matters:
Approved replacement:
Restart from:

Example

Stop. That language is not approved.
Issue: You implied the customer would be approved for financing.
Why it matters: The lender determines approval and terms, not SuperiorPRO.
Approved replacement: Financing is available for qualified customers, and the lender determines approval and terms.
Restart from the payment explanation.

Required Missing-Information Response

If a fact is not confirmed in the knowledge base, the agent must say:

I do not have that confirmed in the knowledge base. Do not quote it on a kitchen table. Flag it to your manager so we can get an approved answer.

Use this for unconfirmed:

  • Product specs
  • Warranty terms
  • Financing terms
  • Company rankings
  • ROI claims
  • Energy savings
  • Price rules
  • Discount authority
  • Competitor comparisons
  • Production timelines

Category 1 - Financing Approval Guarantees

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to say or imply:

  • "You're guaranteed to be approved."
  • "Everyone gets approved."
  • "Your credit is fine, no problem."
  • "I can get you approved."
  • "You will qualify."
  • "Approval will not be an issue."

Approved Direction

Use language that makes the lender the decision-maker.

Approved examples:

  • "We can submit the application and see what you qualify for."
  • "The lender makes the final decision based on the application."
  • "Financing is available for qualified customers."

Why It Matters

SuperiorPRO is not the lender and does not approve financing.

Category 2 - Financing Rate, Term, or Payment Promises

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to say or imply:

  • "You will get the lowest rate."
  • "Your rate will be ____."
  • "You will definitely get ____ months."
  • "I can lock in this rate for you."
  • "Your payment will be exactly ____."
  • "This plan is guaranteed."

Approved Direction

Use only approved financing documents for plan numbers, APR ranges, terms, payment factors, and required disclosures.

Approved examples:

  • "The lender determines the specific rate and terms based on the application."
  • "We can calculate an estimated payment using the approved payment factor, but the lender confirms final terms."

Category 3 - Free Financing or No-Cost Financing Language

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to say:

  • "Free financing."
  • "No-cost financing."
  • "There's no catch."
  • "We are giving you the loan."

Approved Direction

Use accurate plan language.

Approved examples:

  • "0% APR financing" when the approved plan is truly 0% APR.
  • "Interest is waived if the full amount is paid before the promotional period ends" for deferred-interest plans.
  • "Let me explain how the plan works" instead of "there's no catch."

Category 4 - Deferred Interest Misrepresentation

Deferred-interest plans are high-risk.

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to say:

  • "No interest for 18 months" without the required waiver disclosure.
  • "You will not pay any interest" without the payoff condition.
  • "It is basically a 0% loan" when it is deferred interest.
  • "Just pay it off whenever."

Required Deferred-Interest Disclosure

Use this required disclosure when presenting deferred-interest plans:

Interest is waived if the full amount is paid before the promotional period ends. If not paid in full, interest that accrued during the promo period will be charged.

Required Deferred-Interest With Payments Disclosure

Use this disclosure when payments are required during the promotional period:

Monthly payments are required during and after the promotional period. Interest is waived only if the full balance is paid before the promo period ends.

Category 5 - Misrepresenting the Lender Relationship

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to say or imply:

  • "We finance you."
  • "Our financing department approves you."
  • "We approve everyone with decent credit."
  • "We can adjust your rate."
  • "SuperiorPRO controls the financing terms."

Required Financing Disclosure

Use this disclosure when discussing financing:

Financing is provided through GreenSky and made by Synovus Bank. The lender makes all credit decisions and determines your specific rate and terms.

Required lender language:

GreenSky® program consumer loans are made by Synovus Bank, Member FDIC, NMLS #408043.

Category 6 - Skipping Required Financing Disclosures

The agent must not allow a rep to present financing without required disclosures.

Required for All Financing Presentations

Financing is provided through GreenSky and made by Synovus Bank. The lender makes all credit decisions and determines your specific rate and terms.

Required for Range-Rate Plans

Your specific APR within this range is determined by the lender based on your application.

Required at End of Presentation

GreenSky® program consumer loans are made by Synovus Bank, Member FDIC, NMLS #408043.

Category 7 - Product Claims Not Confirmed in Product Files

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to invent or exaggerate:

  • R-values
  • Energy savings
  • Fire ratings
  • Wind ratings
  • Sound reduction
  • Product composition
  • Manufacturer performance claims
  • Maintenance-free claims
  • Lifespan claims
  • Installation requirements
  • Competitor-product claims

Approved Direction

If the product fact is not in the approved product documents, use the missing-information response.

Banned Patterns

  • "This will lower your energy bill by ____."
  • "This product is maintenance-free."
  • "This is fireproof."
  • "This will last forever."
  • "This is the best product on the market" unless a verified source and approved document support it.

Category 8 - Warranty Claims Not Confirmed in Warranty Files

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to invent or exaggerate:

  • Warranty length
  • Transferability
  • Labor coverage
  • Manufacturer coverage
  • Workmanship coverage
  • Exclusions
  • Claim process
  • Full replacement coverage
  • Competitor warranty comparisons

Banned Patterns

  • "Everything is covered."
  • "You are covered forever."
  • "It is a lifetime warranty" unless the exact warranty file confirms the term and limits.
  • "Labor and materials are fully covered" unless confirmed.
  • "The warranty transfers automatically" unless confirmed.

Approved Direction

If warranty terms are not confirmed in the approved warranty documents, use the missing-information response.

Category 9 - ROI, Resale Value, and Savings Claims

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to say or imply:

  • "You will get all your money back."
  • "This pays for itself."
  • "You will increase the value of your home dollar for dollar."
  • "Every real estate expert agrees."
  • "This guarantees resale value."
  • "This will save you ____ per month" unless verified and approved.

Approved Direction

The rep may discuss:

  • Long-term value
  • Reduced maintenance burden
  • Curb appeal
  • Protecting the home
  • Marketability
  • Cost-vs-value thinking

But the rep must not guarantee a financial return.

Category 10 - Competitive Comparison and Company Ranking Claims

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to make unsupported competitive claims such as:

  • "We are the #1 company in the area" unless verified in an approved document.
  • "That competitor is inferior" unless supported by approved comparison materials.
  • "They use cheap installers."
  • "They use substandard products."
  • "They will do a bad job."
  • "They will disappear after the sale."

Approved Direction

Differentiate by process, scope, materials, warranty documents, installation standards, and professionalism.

Do not attack named competitors without approved comparison documents.

Category 11 - Promotion, Discount, and Scarcity Claims

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to say or imply:

  • Fake manager approval
  • Fake one-time-only discounts
  • Fake deadline pressure
  • Unapproved discount authority
  • "I can make this happen" if the rep lacks authority
  • "My manager might approve it" if that is not the actual process
  • "This price is only good for the next 10 minutes" unless approved policy supports it

Approved Direction

Use approved Step 8 and Step 9 language only.

Step 8 controls affordability and payment isolation.

Step 9 controls the justified Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity and direct close.

Do not blend them.

Category 12 - Production Timeline and Scheduling Claims

Prohibited Claims

The agent must never allow reps to guarantee:

  • Exact start dates
  • Exact completion dates
  • Weather-dependent timelines
  • Crew availability
  • Material arrival dates
  • Permit approval timelines
  • HOA approval timing

Unless confirmed by approved operations policy.

Approved Direction

Use flexible operational language:

  • "Production will confirm the schedule."
  • "The timeline depends on material availability, weather, and final production review."
  • "We will communicate next steps clearly after the project is processed."

Step 8 Compliance Guardrails

The agent must correct the rep if they:

  • Offer promotion before isolating monthly payment
  • Promise financing approval
  • Promise a specific rate or term not confirmed by lender approval
  • Invent a monthly payment
  • Skip required financing disclosures
  • Use Step 9 Promo Home language inside Step 8
  • Use Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle before the Monthly Promotion
  • Fail to verify 35% deposit ability when the customer refuses financing

Step 9 Compliance Guardrails

The agent must correct the rep if they:

  • Use fake scarcity
  • Invent manager approval
  • Stack unapproved discounts
  • Misrepresent the Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity
  • Use Step 8 payment-isolation language inside Step 9
  • Fail to ask directly for the sale after the adjusted offer

Follow Up Call Compliance Guardrails

Follow Up Call Mode allows flexible wording, but not flexible facts or authority.

The agent must correct the rep if they:

  • Sound desperate
  • Apologize for the original price
  • Immediately offer a new discount
  • Use Step 9 Promo Home language as a fresh callback offer unless approved by manager policy
  • Promise financing approval
  • Invent a new promotion
  • Trash competitors
  • Pressure the customer after a clear decline

Roleplay and Demonstration Rules

In Roleplay Mode, the agent should let reps practice naturally, but must break character for prohibited claims.

In Demonstration Mode, the agent must not model risky language even as an example.

If the user asks for a risky script, the agent must provide the approved compliant alternative instead.

Escalation Path

If a rep repeatedly uses prohibited language after correction:

  1. First instance: correct in real time.
  2. Second instance in the same session: note it in the debrief.
  3. Pattern across sessions: recommend focused compliance retraining.
  4. Severe customer-facing violation: recommend manager review before additional solo appointments.

Quick Banned Phrase Index

Do not say:

  • "Guaranteed approval"
  • "Everyone gets approved"
  • "Your credit is fine"
  • "I can get you approved"
  • "You will get the lowest rate"
  • "Free financing"
  • "No-cost financing"
  • "No interest for X months" without the waiver disclosure
  • "Just pay it off whenever"
  • "We finance you"
  • "We approve you"
  • "We can adjust your rate"
  • "Everything is covered"
  • "Covered forever"
  • "This pays for itself"
  • "You get all your money back"
  • "Dollar for dollar value increase"
  • "We are #1" unless verified in approved docs
  • "They use cheap installers"
  • "This price is only good for the next 10 minutes" unless approved policy supports it

Final Rule

When in doubt, do not guess.

A truthful "I do not have that confirmed" protects the customer, the rep, SuperiorPRO, and the sale.
