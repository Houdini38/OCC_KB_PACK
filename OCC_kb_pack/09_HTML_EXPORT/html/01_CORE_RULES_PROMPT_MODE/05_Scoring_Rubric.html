Scoring Rubric

Purpose

This document defines how the AI coach scores rep performance during roleplays, transcript reviews, missed-sale diagnosis, and follow-up call practice.

Scoring should be direct, useful, and connected to revenue-producing behavior.

The agent should score what the rep did, not what the rep intended to do.

Scoring Philosophy

The score should help the rep improve one of these outcomes:

  • Close Rate
  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Average Ticket
  • Financing Attachment
  • Margin Discipline
  • Process Compliance
  • Rehash Close Rate
  • Cancellation Reduction
  • Review and Referral Opportunity

If a score does not help improve behavior, it is just noise.

Universal Scoring Scale

Use a 1 to 5 score unless the user requests a different format.

Score Meaning Standard
1 Failed Rep missed the step, created risk, or damaged the opportunity
2 Weak Rep attempted the move but lacked control, clarity, or accuracy
3 Acceptable Rep covered the basics but missed key strength or precision
4 Strong Rep followed the system well with only minor improvement needed
5 Excellent Rep was clear, controlled, accurate, confident, and KPI-focused

Do not give a 5 for friendly tone alone.

A 5 requires process accuracy.

When scoring a full roleplay, transcript, or appointment, use these categories:

Roleplay Scope Scoring

Use this section when the rep practices a focused roleplay lane instead of a full appointment.

The agent must score only the selected roleplay scope unless the rep asks for a broader review.

1. Full Appointment Roleplay Scoring

Score the complete 10-step process.

Required scoring areas:

  • Process Control
  • Trust and Permission
  • Discovery and Urgency
  • Company Differentiation
  • Price Conditioning
  • Product Demonstration
  • Pre-Close
  • Scope Confirmation
  • Step 8 Price Presentation
  • Step 9 Closing Sequence
  • Warm Down
  • Compliance

Primary KPI focus:

  • Close Rate
  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Average Ticket
  • Financing Attachment
  • Margin Discipline
  • Cancellation Reduction

2. Single Step Roleplay Scoring

Score only the selected step.

Required scoring areas:

  • Did the rep stay inside the selected step?
  • Did the rep accomplish the purpose of that step?
  • Did the rep use the correct process flow?
  • Did the rep use approved language if that step has locked language?
  • Did the rep hit the required checkpoint or question?
  • Did the rep avoid drifting into a different step?

Single Step Score: __ / 5
Step Practiced:
Purpose of Step:
Pass / Fail:
KPI Impact:

3. Closing Roleplay Scoring, Steps 8 and 9

Score Step 8 and Step 9 separately.

Do not give one combined close score.

Step 8 Score

Score whether the rep:

  • Presented List Price confidently
  • Presented the high starting monthly payment
  • Asked how the payment fit the budget
  • Isolated monthly comfort
  • Confirmed the payment target
  • Trial-closed List Price
  • Offered the Monthly Promotion at the right time
  • Used Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle only if still needed
  • Avoided Step 9 Promo Home language

Step 9 Score

Score whether the rep:

  • Physically disarmed
  • Asked the timeline question
  • Delivered the WOW line
  • Used the approved Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity language
  • Presented the adjusted offer
  • Asked directly for the sale
  • Isolated the final objection if needed
  • Avoided Step 8 payment-isolation drift

Output format:

Closing Roleplay Score:
Step 8 Score: __ / 5
Step 9 Score: __ / 5

Biggest Strength:
Biggest Drift Risk:
KPI Impact:
Corrected Move:
Repeat Drill:

Automatic score caps:

  • If the rep discounts before isolating payment, Step 8 score cannot exceed 2.
  • If the rep does not ask directly for the sale, Step 9 score cannot exceed 2.
  • If the rep invents financing approval, manager approval, or discount authority, the relevant score cannot exceed 1 until corrected.

4. First Half Roleplay Scoring, Steps 1 through 6

Score only the value-building half of the appointment.

Required scoring areas:

  • Step 1: Did the rep earn permission and set expectations?
  • Step 2: Did the rep create urgency from the inspection?
  • Step 3: Did the rep teach, tailor, and take control with the Company Story?
  • Step 4: Did the rep condition price before price?
  • Step 5: Did the rep make value physical and explain the complete project?
  • Step 6: Did the rep isolate everything except investment?

Primary KPI focus:

  • Close Rate
  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Average Ticket
  • Process Compliance

Output format:

First Half Roleplay Score: __ / 5
Strongest Step:
Weakest Step:
Did the rep earn the right to present price? Yes / No
KPI Impact:
Corrected Move:
Repeat Drill:

Automatic score cap:

If the rep jumps to price before Step 6, First Half Roleplay score cannot exceed 2.

5. Second Half Roleplay Scoring, Steps 6 through 10

Score the transition from pre-close through warm down.

Required scoring areas:

  • Step 6: Did the rep isolate non-price objections before price?
  • Step 7: Did the rep confirm scope clearly before price?
  • Step 8: Did the rep follow the approved payment-control sequence?
  • Step 9: Did the rep follow the approved close sequence?
  • Step 10: Did the rep reinforce the decision and confirm next steps?

Primary KPI focus:

  • Same-Day Close Rate
  • Financing Attachment
  • Margin
  • Cancellation Reduction
  • Follow-Up Quality

Output format:

Second Half Roleplay Score: __ / 5
Step 6 Score:
Step 8 Score:
Step 9 Score:
Biggest Miss:
KPI Impact:
Corrected Move:
Repeat Drill:

Automatic score caps:

  • If the rep enters Step 8 with unresolved non-price objections, Second Half Roleplay score cannot exceed 3.
  • If the rep discounts before isolating monthly payment, Step 8 score cannot exceed 2.
  • If the rep does not ask directly for the sale, Step 9 score cannot exceed 2.
  • If the rep gets the sale but skips Warm Down, Step 10 score cannot exceed 3.

Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric

Step 1 - Warm-Up

Score 5

Rep clearly sets the agenda, confirms appointment length, confirms decision-makers, and earns permission to run the full process.

Score 3

Rep is friendly and professional but does not fully control the agenda or confirm timing/decision-makers.

Score 1

Rep rushes into inspection, product, or price without setting expectations.

KPI Impact

Process Compliance and Close Rate.

Step 2 - Pre-Assessment / Inspection

Score 5

Rep walks the home with the homeowner, points out specific issues, creates urgency, and gets the homeowner to agree the problem should be addressed.

Score 3

Rep performs the inspection but does not strongly involve the homeowner or create urgency.

Score 1

Rep inspects silently or fails to connect the problem to consequence.

KPI Impact

Same-Day Close Rate and Close Rate.

Step 3 - Company Story / Challenger Method

Score 5

Rep teaches the Three Ways to Do Business, tailors risk to the home, and explains why SuperiorPRO is the safer complete-scope choice.

Score 3

Rep gives a basic company story but does not challenge the homeowner's decision framework.

Score 1

Rep gives a generic pitch, skips differentiation, or competitor-bashes.

KPI Impact

Close Rate and Shopping Resistance.

Step 4 - Investment / Price Conditioning

Score 5

Rep frames the project as an investment, explains cost-vs-value, and conditions price before price is shown.

Score 3

Rep mentions value but does not create a clear investment frame.

Score 1

Rep skips price conditioning or reveals price too early.

KPI Impact

Average Ticket and Close Rate.

Step 5 - Product Demonstration

Score 5

Rep makes value physical, explains the complete project, connects product/material/labor to the homeowner's problem, and confirms understanding.

Score 3

Rep shows samples or features but does not fully explain complete project value.

Score 1

Rep gives a weak demo, talks too generally, or fails to connect value to the home.

KPI Impact

Average Ticket and Same-Day Close.

Step 6 - Pre-Close

Score 5

Rep confirms custom-order logic, confirms when-not-if, and isolates all non-price objections before price.

Score 3

Rep asks some pre-close questions but does not fully isolate non-price concerns.

Score 1

Rep goes to price with unresolved trust, product, scope, timing, or decision-maker concerns.

KPI Impact

Same-Day Close Rate and Objection Isolation.

Step 7 - Paperwork / Scope Confirmation

Score 5

Rep confirms materials, labor, options, colors, exclusions, and the full scope before pricing.

Score 3

Rep completes paperwork but does not use it to build scope confidence.

Score 1

Rep treats paperwork as admin or leaves the scope vague.

KPI Impact

Production Accuracy, Cancellation Reduction, and Process Compliance.

Step 8 - Price Presentation

Score 5

Rep follows the approved Step 8 sequence: List Price, starting monthly payment, budget-fit question, monthly isolation, payment confirmation, List Price trial close, Monthly Promotion, and Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle only if still needed.

Score 3

Rep presents price and payment but skips one control point or weakly isolates monthly comfort.

Score 1

Rep offers promotion too early, defends total price too long, skips payment isolation, or creates Step 8 / Step 9 drift.

KPI Impact

Financing Attachment, Margin, Close Rate, and Same-Day Close.

Automatic Score Cap

If the rep offers a discount before isolating monthly payment, Step 8 score cannot exceed 2.

If the rep promises financing approval or terms, Step 8 score cannot exceed 1 until corrected.

Step 9 - Closing Sequence

Score 5

Rep follows the approved Step 9 sequence: physical disarm, timeline question, WOW line, justified Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity, adjusted offer, direct close, and final objection isolation if needed.

Score 3

Rep asks for the sale but misses a required control move such as the disarm, timeline question, or WOW line.

Score 1

Rep skips the approved close sequence, blends Step 8 language into Step 9, stacks unapproved discounts, or never clearly asks for the sale.

KPI Impact

Same-Day Close Rate and Margin.

Automatic Score Cap

If the rep does not ask directly for the sale, Step 9 score cannot exceed 2.

If the rep invents manager approval or unapproved discount authority, Step 9 score cannot exceed 1 until corrected.

Step 10 - Warm Down

Score 5

Rep reinforces the decision, confirms next steps, reduces buyer's remorse, and opens the door for review/referral at the right time.

Score 3

Rep confirms some next steps but rushes the emotional warm down.

Score 1

Rep gets the signature and leaves quickly, creating buyer's remorse risk.

KPI Impact

Cancellation Reduction, Reviews, Referrals, and Customer Experience.

Objection Handling Scoring

Use this when reviewing how a rep handled an objection.

Score Area What to Listen For
Objection Isolation Did the rep identify the real concern?
Step Placement Did the rep handle the objection in the correct part of the system?
Customer-Specific Tie-Back Did the rep use the homeowner's actual words or situation?
Approved Response Did the rep stay inside approved language and guardrails?
Close-Back Question Did the rep ask for a decision or next step after responding?
KPI Awareness Did the rep understand what the objection affected?

Automatic Score Caps

  • If the rep answers a vague objection without isolating it, objection score cannot exceed 3.
  • If the rep discounts before isolating the objection, objection score cannot exceed 2.
  • If the rep uses a prohibited claim, objection score cannot exceed 1 until corrected.

Follow Up Call Scoring

Follow Up Call Mode is not a script test.

Score the rep on outcomes, not memorized wording.

Score Area What to Listen For
Professional Opening Did the rep sound confident and purposeful?
Original Appointment Reference Did the rep accurately reference the original appointment?
Current Status Check Did the rep find out where the customer stands now?
Objection Isolation Did the rep isolate what is still holding the customer back?
Value Reconnection Did the rep reconnect to the original problem and urgency?
Presentation Discipline Did the rep avoid restarting the whole presentation?
Margin Discipline Did the rep avoid panic discounting?
Clear Ask Did the rep ask for a decision or scheduled next action?
CRM Quality Did the rep identify the true objection and next step?

Score 5

Rep sounds professional, references the original appointment, isolates the current barrier, reconnects to the problem, avoids panic discounting, and earns a decision or specific next action.

Score 3

Rep has a decent conversation but does not strongly isolate the current barrier or ask for a clear next step.

Score 1

Rep says "just checking in," avoids the real objection, restarts the whole presentation, or immediately discounts.

KPI Impact

Rehash Close Rate, Overall Close Rate, Same-Week Recovery Rate, Margin Discipline, and CRM Follow-Up Quality.

Compliance Scoring

Compliance issues override normal scoring.

Automatic Compliance Fail

A rep receives an automatic compliance fail if they:

  • Promise financing approval
  • Invent APR, term, payment, or lender decision
  • Make unsupported product claims
  • Make unsupported warranty claims
  • Make unsupported ROI or resale value claims
  • Create false scarcity
  • Invent manager approval
  • Invent discount authority
  • Make unverified competitor claims

If this happens, the agent must stop the roleplay or review and correct the language.

Final Rule

The score should never be decorative.

Every score must tell the rep what to fix and why it matters to revenue.
