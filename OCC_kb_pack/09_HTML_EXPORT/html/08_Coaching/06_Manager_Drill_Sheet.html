Manager Drill Sheet

Purpose

This document is for sales managers, trainers, and ride-along leaders running weekly sales meetings, team drills, or post-appointment debriefs.

This file is manager-facing. It should not override rep-facing approved language, Step 8, Step 9, financing, warranty, or product facts.

Coaching Philosophy

  1. Score process, not cleverness.
  2. Drill one step at a time.
  3. Tie every drill to a KPI.
  4. Use real photos and real customer language.
  5. Reward clean process.
  6. Coach the upstream cause, not only the downstream objection.
  7. Protect margin and customer trust before style.

Drill Menu

Step 1 Timer Drill

Rep opens the appointment and confirms time expectation.

Pass standard: agenda is clear, appointment length is confirmed, and rep does not jump into product or price.

Step 2 Urgency Drill

Use real inspection photos. Rep gets customer to own the problem.

Pass standard: customer agrees the issue is real, worsening, and worth addressing.

Step 4 Investment Drill

Rep explains cost versus value without giving price.

Pass standard: uses investment language and earns agreement before demo.

Step 6 Isolation Drill

Rep delivers Pre-Close and isolates anything besides investment.

Pass standard: rep asks the approved isolation question clearly before price.

Step 8 Approved Sequence Drill

Customer reacts to price. Rep must run Step 8 in approved order.

Pass standard: List Price, high starting payment, monthly isolation, confirmation, trial close, Monthly Promotion, then Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle only if needed.

Step 9 Approved Close Drill

Rep practices physical disarm, timeline question, WOW line, Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity, adjusted offer, direct close.

Pass standard: no skipped step, no fake manager authority, no Step 8 language drift.

Follow-Up Call Drill

Rep calls a no-sale customer.

Pass standard: rep reopens professionally, references appointment, isolates current blocker, reconnects to original problem, and asks for decision or scheduled next action.

Manager Debrief Questions

  1. Did the rep follow the 10-step sequence?
  2. Did the customer emotionally own the problem in Step 2?
  3. Did the rep price-condition before price?
  4. Did the rep run the Step 6 isolation question?
  5. Did the rep deliver the approved Step 8 sequence?
  6. Did the rep use approved Step 9 sequence and direct close?
  7. Did the rep avoid invented financing, warranty, product, ROI, or manager authority claims?
  8. If no sale, did the rep leave with a defined next step?
  9. Did the rep complete the Warm Down after a yes?
  10. What is the single behavior this rep should drill with Hope this week?

Final Rule

Managers should observe the downstream symptom, then coach the upstream cause.

That is how coaching moves revenue instead of creating prettier scripts.
