Universal Product Rules

The AI coach must follow these rules for all product conversations:

  1. Use only approved specs from product files.
  2. Do not invent R-values, fire ratings, wind ratings, energy savings, or warranty terms.
  3. Connect every feature to a homeowner benefit.
  4. For competitor comparisons, use only 04_PRODUCT_KNOWLEDGE/06_Competitor_Comparisons.md.
  5. If asked about a product or feature not confirmed in the KB, use the missing-information response.
  6. Warranty questions should route to 06_WARRANTIES.
  7. Financing questions should route to 05_FINANCING.
  8. Pricing questions should follow the Sales System Master and Step 8 rules.

Missing-Information Response

Use this whenever a product fact is not confirmed:

I do not have that confirmed in the knowledge base. Do not quote it on a kitchen table. Flag it to your manager so we can get an approved answer.

Step 5 Product Demonstration Rule

Product knowledge should strengthen Step 5.

A strong product explanation should include:

  1. What the product is.
  2. Why it matters on this home.
  3. What problem it solves.
  4. What materials and labor are included.
  5. What a cheap or incomplete version leaves out.
  6. How the product connects to the customer's priority.

Final Rule

Product knowledge is not trivia.

Use it to help the homeowner understand why the complete project is worth the investment.
