Overview

SuperiorPRO installs ProVia entry doors and Simonton replacement windows.

Windows and doors should be positioned around comfort, efficiency, security, appearance, and proper installation.

The product matters, but measurement, fit, insulation, sealing, trim, and installation quality are what make the project perform.

Homeowner-Facing Window Install Process

Use this as a general explanation. Actual scope may vary by contract.

  1. Inspect existing window opening for rot, sagging, or sealing concerns.
  2. Remove the old window where included.
  3. Repair sash, trim, or framing where included in the scope.
  4. Install the new window square, level, and plumb.
  5. Insulate and seal gaps.
  6. Apply trim, caulk, and finish details according to scope.
  7. Test operation and complete final walk-through.

Homeowner-Facing Door Install Process

Use this as a general explanation. Actual scope may vary by contract.

  1. Measure the existing opening.
  2. Remove the old door and frame where included.
  3. Inspect threshold and framing.
  4. Repair damaged areas according to the contract scope.
  5. Install the new pre-hung unit.
  6. Insulate, seal, and trim.
  7. Install hardware and test operation.
  8. Complete final inspection and walk-through.

Approved Talking Points - Windows

Use these to build value without overpromising:

  • "A replacement window is only as good as the measurement and installation."
  • "The goal is not just new glass. The goal is a tighter, better-looking, better-operating opening."
  • "Proper sealing and insulation around the window matter as much as the window itself."
  • "Comfort, operation, appearance, and reduced drafts are usually what homeowners notice first."

Approved Talking Points - Doors

Use these to build value without overpromising:

  • "An entry door changes curb appeal, security feel, and daily operation all at once."
  • "The door slab matters, but so do the frame, threshold, hardware, insulation, and seal."
  • "A poorly installed door can leak air and water even if the door itself is high quality."
  • "With doors, fit and installation are just as important as the product brand."

Step 5 Demo Guidance

A strong windows and doors demo should cover:

  1. Current problem: drafts, rot, sticking, leaks, security, appearance, or operation.
  2. Product selected and why it fits the home.
  3. Measurement and custom-fit process.
  4. Installation, insulation, sealing, trim, and cleanup.
  5. What a cheap or incomplete installation leaves out.

Approved bridge:

The product is important, but windows and doors fail when the opening is measured wrong, sealed wrong, or finished poorly. That is why the full installation process matters.

Banned Claims

Do not say:

  • "Hurricane proof."
  • "Bulletproof."
  • "Stops 100% of UV."
  • "Stops 100% of heat."
  • "Soundproof."
  • "Pays for itself in X years."
  • "This will cut your energy bill by X%."
  • "Lifetime warranty" unless the exact product component and manufacturer language support it.
  • "Everything is covered."

Top Customer Questions

Customer: "How much will I save on energy?"

Approved response:

Energy savings vary based on your home's insulation, HVAC system, current windows, and habits. What we can say is that better windows and proper installation can improve comfort, reduce drafts, and help the home perform better.

Customer: "Do you do custom sizes?"

Approved response:

Yes, replacement windows and doors are measured for the opening. We measure, confirm the scope, and order based on the approved project details.

Customer: "Can I replace just one window?"

Approved response:

That depends on project minimums and economics. Let me confirm current policy before giving a firm answer.

Customer: "Will my house be open during install?"

Approved response:

Openings are managed one at a time and coordinated around weather and schedule. The crew removes, installs, seals, and secures each opening as part of the process.

Customer: "What warranty do I get?"

Approved response:

Warranty depends on the specific product and written warranty documents. SuperiorPRO generally covers installation issues for 24 months, and manufacturer coverage may apply depending on the product.
