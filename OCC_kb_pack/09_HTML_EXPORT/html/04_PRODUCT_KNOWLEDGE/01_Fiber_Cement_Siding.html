Homeowner-Facing Install Process

Use this as a general explanation of the complete project. Actual scope may vary by contract.

  1. Remove existing siding where included in the project scope.
  2. Inspect the substrate and sheathing.
  3. Identify and address rotted or damaged areas according to the contract scope.
  4. Install weather barrier / house wrap as specified.
  5. Install fiber cement siding to manufacturer specifications.
  6. Caulk, seal, prime, or finish according to project specifications.
  7. Paint or finish where included.
  8. Complete cleanup, walk-through, and punch list.

Why It Beats Vinyl

Use category comparisons, not attacks.

Fiber cement can be positioned against vinyl by explaining:

  • It is thicker and more rigid than typical vinyl siding.
  • It does not melt or warp the same way vinyl can under extreme heat exposure.
  • It has a more substantial look and feel.
  • It is a premium cladding option for homeowners who want long-term curb appeal.
  • It is less vulnerable to pest damage than wood.

Guardrail

Do not claim it is the same price as vinyl.

Fiber cement is a premium product and should be presented as a value investment, not a cheap alternative.

Why It Beats Wood

Fiber cement can be positioned against wood by explaining:

  • It resists rot better than wood.
  • It is not a food source for termites.
  • Woodpeckers cannot drill into it the way they can with wood siding.
  • It reduces the maintenance cycle associated with wood siding.
  • It provides a more durable exterior option when installed correctly.

Top Customer Questions

Customer: "How is it different from vinyl?"

Approved response:

Fiber cement is thicker, more rigid, and more durable than typical vinyl. It has a more substantial look, resists pests better than wood, and does not melt or warp the same way vinyl can under extreme heat exposure.

Customer: "Will it crack?"

Approved response:

Proper installation is what protects the product. Any exterior product can fail if installed incorrectly, which is why installation method matters so much.

Customer: "What about repainting?"

Approved response:

Repainting depends on the finish option and maintenance over time. Let me check the current product and warranty documents before quoting a specific finish term.

Customer: "Is it heavy? Will it damage my house?"

Approved response:

Fiber cement is heavier than vinyl, but it is designed for residential siding installation when properly fastened to the structure. We inspect the surface and install according to the project scope and manufacturer requirements.

Customer: "What warranty do I get?"

Approved response:

Warranty depends on the product and finish. SuperiorPRO generally covers installation issues for 24 months, and James Hardie may provide manufacturer coverage where it applies. The written warranty document is the final word.
