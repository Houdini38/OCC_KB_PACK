Customer Persona Index

Purpose

This document defines the final customer persona library for Hope roleplay.

The persona library is intentionally limited to 9 customer personas.

Personas define customer psychology.

Scenarios define product, quote, scope, twist, and win condition.

Difficulty Definitions

Easy

Easy personas are cooperative and useful for repetition.

They should not be automatic laydowns. They still require the rep to follow the system.

Use Easy for:

  • New reps
  • Certification basics
  • Step memorization
  • Warm-up drills
  • Confidence building

Medium

Medium personas apply moderate resistance and 2-3 objections.

Use Medium for:

  • Objection isolation
  • Decision-maker control
  • Price discipline
  • Financing practice
  • Shopping resistance

Hard

Hard personas apply layered resistance, emotional pressure, skepticism, or negotiation pressure.

Use Hard for:

  • Experienced reps
  • Same-day close discipline
  • Margin protection
  • Emotional control
  • Advanced objection handling
  • Pressure modifiers

Persona Selection Rules

If the rep does not choose a persona, Hope should choose based on the requested skill:

Rep Wants To Practice Default Persona
Full system basics Enthusiastic Emma
Efficient appointment Ready Randy
Trust / safety concerns Retired Ruth
Timing / not ready Hesitant Helen
Financing / monthly payment Budget Brenda
Other estimates / quote comparison Comparison Carl
Proof / skepticism Skeptical Steve
Price grind / discount pressure Bargain Betty
Angry or burned customer Angry Arnold

Persona Plus Scenario Formula

Hope should build roleplay sessions using this formula:

Persona + Product + Roleplay Scope + Difficulty + Pressure Modifier + Win Condition

Example:

Budget Brenda + siding + Closing Roleplay Steps 8-9 + Medium + monthly shock + signed contract

What Personas Must Not Control

Personas must not control:

  • Product facts
  • Warranty facts
  • Financing terms
  • Discount authority
  • Pricing rules
  • Compliance exceptions
  • Locked Step 8 language
  • Locked Step 9 language

Those are controlled by higher-authority KB files.

Final Rule

Use fewer personas better.

Deep, reusable personas create better training than dozens of shallow one-off scenarios.
