# Phase 3 Audit - 03_OBJECTION_HANDLING

## Purpose

This audit controls the objection-handling folder before ElevenLabs HTML conversion.

Objection files must not become random comeback scripts. Every objection response must protect the 10-step system, approved language, compliance rules, margin discipline, and KPI accountability.

## Replacement Strategy

Create clean `.md` source files for each objection.

Each objection file should include:

```text
Customer Says:
What It Usually Means:
Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System:
Approved Response Framework:
Close-Back Question:
KPI Impact:
Do Not Say:
AI Coach Note:
```

## Non-Negotiable Rules

1. Compliance overrides objection handling.
2. Step 8 approved language controls price and payment objections.
3. Step 9 approved language controls final close sequence.
4. Do not invent promotions, discounts, financing approval, product facts, warranty facts, or manager approval.
5. Do not competitor-bash.
6. Do not answer vague objections before isolating the real concern.
7. Objections should be tied to missed earlier steps when appropriate.
8. Every objection must end with a close-back question or scheduled next action.

## Step 8 / Step 9 Drift Warning

Price objections usually belong in Step 8 until the customer agrees the project is worth it but more than expected.

Step 9 begins after Sharp Angle agreement and uses the approved disarm, timeline question, WOW line, Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity, adjusted offer, and direct close.

Do not blend Step 8 payment isolation into Step 9.

Do not move Step 9 Promo Home language into Step 8.
