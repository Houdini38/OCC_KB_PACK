# Warranty Comparison FAQ

## Purpose

This document gives reps simple answers to common customer warranty questions.

The goal is to build trust through clarity, not overwhelm the customer with legal detail.

This file supports the Warranty Overview and Manufacturer Warranties by Product files. The signed contract and written warranty document are always the final word.

---

# Coaching Philosophy

Warranty coaching should be low-interruption.

Hope should not stop a roleplay every time a rep explains a warranty imperfectly.

Use soft coaching after the attempt unless the rep clearly overpromises.

Hard correction is only needed when the rep creates a false warranty expectation.

---

# Safe Default Warranty Answer

Use this any time the rep is unsure:

> SuperiorPRO warranties vary by product. We cover installation issues for 24 months on most jobs. Some products also have longer manufacturer coverage where we help with labor if the manufacturer approves. Your signed contract and warranty document are the final word.

---

# Common Customer Questions

## Customer: "What kind of warranty do I get?"

Approved answer:

> You get 24 months on our workmanship plus manufacturer coverage where it applies. I will show you the exact warranty page before you sign.

## Customer: "Is everything covered?"

Approved answer:

> No warranty covers everything. We stand behind our installation and help with manufacturer issues we can. Things like storms, normal wear, cleaning, and caulking are not covered.

## Customer: "So this is a 30-year warranty?" for Hardie

Approved answer:

> The Hardie product itself has a 30-year substrate warranty from James Hardie. Our workmanship is covered for 24 months. That is the honest way to explain it.

## Customer: "What voids the warranty?"

Approved answer:

> Non-payment, letting another contractor work on it, or damage from storms, misuse, or harsh cleaners. Keep your paperwork and we will take care of you within the warranty terms.

## Customer: "Does the warranty transfer if I sell the house?"

Approved answer:

> Transfer rules depend on the product. Let me check the product-specific warranty page so I give you the right answer.

Then use the product-specific table in `06_WARRANTIES/02_Manufacturer_Warranties.md`.

## Customer: "What happens if the manufacturer approves a claim?"

Approved answer:

> If the manufacturer approves a covered issue and provides the material, SuperiorPRO helps with the labor according to the warranty terms. The exact process depends on the product and written warranty.

## Customer: "What if SuperiorPRO is not around in 20 years?"

Approved answer:

> That is one reason manufacturer warranty matters. The manufacturer warranty is separate from our workmanship warranty. SuperiorPRO covers our installation for 24 months, and the manufacturer may provide longer product coverage depending on the product and approval.

## Customer: "Do you cover labor for the full manufacturer warranty?"

Approved answer:

> We help with labor on manufacturer issues when the manufacturer approves the claim and supplies covered material. The exact labor coverage depends on the product and written warranty.

# Warranty Comparison Talking Points

Use these to explain the difference between SuperiorPRO and a contractor with only their own warranty.

## Contractor-Only Warranty

A contractor-only warranty depends entirely on that contractor still being available, willing, and able to honor it.

## SuperiorPRO Warranty Structure

SuperiorPRO offers:

1. SuperiorPRO workmanship / installation coverage for 24 months on most jobs.
2. Manufacturer warranty where applicable.
3. Labor help on manufacturer issues when the manufacturer approves and supplies covered material.

Approved talking point:

> Most contractors offer their own warranty. We offer the manufacturer's warranty where it applies and ours, which gives you two layers to look at instead of only one.

---

# What Not To Say

Do not say:

- "Everything is covered."
- "You are covered forever."
- "This is a lifetime warranty" unless the manufacturer specifically warrants that product or component for life.
- "SuperiorPRO gives you a 30-year workmanship warranty."
- "Labor is covered for the entire manufacturer warranty no matter what."
- "The manufacturer will definitely approve the claim."
- "No one else has a warranty like this" unless verified.

---

# Hope Coaching Rules

## Soft Correction

Use soft correction when the rep is mostly right but unclear.

Example:

```text
Good direction. Tighten it by separating our workmanship coverage from the manufacturer coverage. SuperiorPRO covers installation issues for 24 months. Manufacturer coverage may last longer depending on the product and approval.
```

## Hard Correction

Use hard correction only when the rep overpromises.

Example:

```text
Pause. That overpromises the warranty.
Issue: You said everything is covered.
Corrected language: No warranty covers everything. SuperiorPRO covers workmanship for 24 months, and manufacturer coverage depends on the product and written terms.
```
