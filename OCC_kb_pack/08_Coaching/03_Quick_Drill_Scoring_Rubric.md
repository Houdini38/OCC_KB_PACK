# Quick Drill Scoring Rubric

## Purpose

This rubric controls short-form drill scoring.

Use this rubric for:

- Objection drills
- Step 6 Pre-Close isolation drills
- Step 8 payment-isolation drills
- Step 9 final-objection drills
- Follow-up call micro-drills when a point score is requested

Do not use this rubric for full roleplay sessions. Use `08_Coaching/00_Roleplay_Scoring_Rubric.md` for full roleplays.

---

# Quick Drill Scoring Categories

Total score: 100 points.

| # | Category | Points | What It Measures |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Acknowledges calmly | 10 | No defensiveness, no rushing past it |
| 2 | Isolates root concern | 20 | Diagnoses before answering |
| 3 | Uses approved system structure | 20 | Follows approved sequence and wording where locked |
| 4 | Ties back to homeowner problem | 15 | Connects response to inspection, urgency, or stated need |
| 5 | Uses urgency / incentive correctly | 15 | No fake scarcity, no invented promotion |
| 6 | Closes back clearly | 10 | Asks for decision or defined next step |
| 7 | Delivery | 10 | Confident, concise, no filler spiral |
|  | **Total** | **100** |  |

---

# Automatic Zero / Cap Rules

Automatic zero for approved-structure category if the rep:

- Invents APR, term, payment, warranty, product spec, ROI, or manager authority
- Uses fake urgency
- Skips required sales sequence in a locked step

Hope should only interrupt immediately for serious customer-risk issues. Normal wording drift is coached in the debrief.

---

# Score Ranges

| Total | Tier | Hope's Next Step |
|---:|---|---|
| 90-100 | Table-Ready | Move to harder customer or pressure modifier. |
| 80-89 | Close | One targeted correction, then repeat three times. |
| 70-79 | Needs Work | Drill the lowest category separately. |
| 60-69 | Foundation Gap | Return to Coaching Mode on the weakest category. |
| Below 60 | Upstream Failure | Diagnose Step 2, Step 4, Step 6, or Step 8 before more objection scripts. |

# Final Rule

Quick Drill scoring rewards clean, repeatable behavior.

Do not let a clever answer outrank correct sequence.
