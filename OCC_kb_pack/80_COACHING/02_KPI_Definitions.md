# KPI Definitions

## Purpose

This document defines the KPIs Hope coaches against.

Every meaningful correction should tie to a measurable field outcome.

---

# Primary Sales KPIs

## Close Rate

Definition:

```text
Signed contracts ÷ qualified appointments
```

SuperiorPRO standard:

```text
25% minimum
```

Why it matters:

Close Rate shows whether the rep can convert qualified appointments into sold projects.

What moves it:

- Following the 10-step sales system
- Strong discovery
- Clear value building
- Objection isolation
- Direct close ask
- Strong follow-up discipline

---

## Revenue Per Appointment / RPA

Definition:

```text
Sold revenue ÷ qualified appointments
```

SuperiorPRO standard:

```text
$3,000 minimum
```

Why it matters:

RPA combines close rate and average ticket into one revenue productivity metric.

What moves it:

- Higher close rate
- Higher average ticket
- Better scope control
- Less early discounting
- Better financing usage when appropriate

---

## Same-Day Close Rate

Definition:

```text
Sales closed during the appointment ÷ total sales
```

SuperiorPRO standard:

```text
70% minimum
75% target
80% elite
```

Why it matters:

Same-day close rate measures how often reps convert while urgency, emotion, decision-makers, and value are still active.

What moves it:

- Strong Step 2 urgency
- Strong Step 6 isolation
- Clean Step 8 payment control
- Correct Step 9 close sequence
- Direct ask for the sale

---

## Average Ticket

Definition:

```text
Total signed dollars ÷ number of contracts
```

Standard:

```text
Track by rep, product, and lead source.
```

Why it matters:

Average Ticket reflects project completeness, scope discipline, value building, and discount control.

What moves it:

- Step 4 price conditioning
- Step 5 complete project demonstration
- Step 7 scope clarity
- Step 8 margin discipline
- Proper cross-sell when relevant

---

## Financing Attachment Rate

Definition:

```text
Contracts using financing ÷ total contracts
```

Standard:

```text
Track by rep and product. Coach trend, not one-off usage.
```

Why it matters:

Financing is the affordability lever. Strong usage helps reps solve monthly payment resistance without relying only on discount.

What moves it:

- Presenting monthly payment in Step 8
- Isolating monthly comfort zone
- Using approved plan math
- Avoiding prohibited financing claims
- Explaining lender role correctly

---

## Process Compliance Score

Definition:

```text
Average score on process-compliance dimension across roleplays, drills, transcript reviews, and ride-alongs
```

SuperiorPRO standard:

```text
4.0+ average or 90%+ compliant appointments
```

Why it matters:

Process compliance is the controllable behavior that drives close rate, RPA, margin, and same-day close.

What moves it:

- Single-step drills
- Roleplay reps
- Manager ride-alongs
- Fast correction of skipped steps
- Step 8 / Step 9 drift prevention

---

# Secondary KPIs

## One-Leg Conversion Rate

Definition:

```text
One-leg appointments that result in same-day close or firm rescheduled appointment with all decision-makers
```

Why it matters:

One-leg appointments create decision-maker risk. The rep's job is to either convert ethically or secure the right next step.

What moves it:

- Step 1 decision-maker confirmation
- Asking who else is involved
- Not presenting final price to an unqualified decision setup when inappropriate
- Clear next-step scheduling

---

## Rehash Close Rate

Definition:

```text
No-sale appointments that later convert through follow-up or rehash process
```

Why it matters:

Rehash recovers pipeline that would otherwise die.

What moves it:

- Accurate loss reason
- Clear next step
- Follow-Up Call Mode outcomes
- Isolating remaining objection
- Avoiding panic discounting

---

## Discount Discipline

Definition:

```text
Average discount given relative to List Price and approved plan authority
```

Why it matters:

Discounting affects margin. Early discounting also trains customers to negotiate before value is fully understood.

What moves it:

- Step 4 price conditioning
- Step 8 payment isolation
- Correct Monthly Promotion sequence
- Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle discipline
- Approved Step 9 Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity framing

---

## Demo Completion Rate

Definition:

```text
Appointments where the full product demonstration and complete project explanation were completed
```

Target:

```text
95%+
```

Why it matters:

Skipped or shallow demos produce price objections because the customer never fully understood the complete project.

What moves it:

- Step 5 discipline
- Product knowledge
- Explaining materials, prep, labor, installation, cleanup, and warranty boundaries

| Rep Mistake | KPI Affected |

| Skipping Step 1 decision-maker confirmation | Close Rate / One-Leg Conversion |

| Weak Step 3 company differentiation | Close Rate / Shopping Resistance |
| Weak Step 4 price conditioning | Average Ticket / Margin |
| Weak Step 5 demo | Average Ticket / Same-Day Close Rate |
| Skipping Step 6 isolation | Same-Day Close Rate / Objection Handling |
| Weak Step 7 scope confirmation | Cancellation Reduction / Production Accuracy |
| Defending List Price too long | Financing Attachment / Same-Day Close Rate |
| Discounting before isolating monthly | Margin / RPA |
| Skipping Step 9 disarm | Same-Day Close Rate |
| No direct close ask | Close Rate / Same-Day Close Rate |
| Weak follow-up call | Rehash Close Rate |
| Financing compliance violation | Compliance / Customer Trust / Business Risk |

# Hope's KPI Reporting Rule

Hope should connect corrections to KPIs in plain language.

Example:

```text
Your Step 8 score was a 3. You defended the total investment instead of moving to monthly comfort. KPI impact: financing attachment and same-day close rate. Drill the redirect until it is automatic.
```
