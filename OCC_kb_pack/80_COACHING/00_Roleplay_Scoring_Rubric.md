# Roleplay Scoring Rubric

## Purpose

This rubric controls how Hope grades full roleplays and multi-step roleplays.

Use this for:

- Full Appointment Roleplay
- First Half Roleplay
- Second Half Roleplay
- Closing Roleplay when a 5-part scorecard is requested
- Transcript review
- Diagnostics Mode

Do not use this for short objection drills. Use `80_COACHING/03_Quick_Drill_Scoring_Rubric.md` for quick drills.

---

# Required Debrief Format

Every roleplay debrief must include:

1. Scorecard
2. One specific strength
3. One priority fix
4. KPI impact
5. Corrected move
6. Repeat drill

Default format:

```text
Score:
Process Compliance: __ / 5
Discovery / Urgency: __ / 5
Value Building: __ / 5
Objection Handling: __ / 5
Closing: __ / 5
Overall: __ / 5

Specific Strength:
Priority Fix:
KPI Impact:
Corrected Move:
Repeat Drill:
```

Hope should not list five fixes unless the manager asks for a full audit.

---

# Dimension 1 - Process Compliance

What it measures:

Did the rep follow the SuperiorPRO 10-Step Sales System without skipping, shortcutting, or blending steps?

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | All required steps completed in sequence with no major drift. |
| 4 | Process mostly complete with one minor weakness or delivery issue. |
| 3 | One full step missed, rushed, or significantly weakened. |
| 2 | Multiple steps missed or major sequencing issues. |
| 1 | Major process failure or rep tried to close without earning the close. |

Automatic caps:

- If Step 6 is skipped before price, Process Compliance cannot exceed 3.
- If Step 8 and Step 9 are blended, Process Compliance cannot exceed 3.
- If rep invents financing, warranty, product, ROI, or manager authority, compliance rules override scoring.

KPI impact:

- Close Rate
- Same-Day Close Rate
- Process Compliance

---

# Dimension 2 - Discovery / Urgency

What it measures:

Did the rep uncover the customer's emotional driver, decision dynamics, timing, and problem ownership?

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | Customer emotionally owns the problem and urgency is tied to the home. |
| 4 | Strong discovery with one missing detail. |
| 3 | Functional discovery only; emotional driver is shallow. |
| 2 | Mostly yes/no questions; urgency not created. |
| 1 | No real discovery; rep went straight to pitch or price. |

Watch for:

- Past-Future-Present
- Decision-maker confirmation
- Customer's own words
- Inspection-based urgency

KPI impact:

- Close Rate
- Same-Day Close Rate
- Rehash Close Rate

---

# Dimension 3 - Value Building

What it measures:

Did the rep create value before price through Company Story, Investment / Price Conditioning, and Product Demonstration?

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | Three Ways, cost-vs-value, product demo, materials, labor, and complete project value all connect to the customer's home. |
| 4 | Strong value build with one generic section. |
| 3 | Product shown but value is only partly connected to homeowner priorities. |
| 2 | Generic product pitch or weak price conditioning. |
| 1 | No meaningful value build before price. |

Watch for:

- Three Ways to Do Business
- Cost versus value before price
- Complete project explanation
- Materials and labor explained
- Product facts stay inside approved KB

KPI impact:

- Average Ticket
- Close Rate
- Margin Discipline

---

# Dimension 4 - Objection Handling

What it measures:

Did the rep isolate the real objection, respond with approved system logic, tie back to the customer's problem, and close back?

Required framework:

```text
Acknowledge
Isolate
Clarify
Respond
Tie Back
Close Back
```

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | Real objection isolated and handled cleanly with a direct close-back. |
| 4 | Objection handled, but one part was light. |
| 3 | Rep answered surface objection without fully isolating. |
| 2 | Rep defended, overexplained, or stacked rebuttals. |
| 1 | Rep folded, argued, or created a new objection. |

KPI impact:

- Same-Day Close Rate
- Close Rate
- Rehash Close Rate

---

# Dimension 5 - Closing

What it measures:

Did the rep deliver Step 8 and Step 9 correctly without early discounting, weak ask, or language drift?

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | Step 8 and Step 9 sequences are clean, direct, and compliant. |
| 4 | Sequence correct with one minor delivery weakness. |
| 3 | Some elements present but rushed or out of order. |
| 2 | Early discounting, weak isolation, no disarm, no WOW line, or indirect close. |
| 1 | No real close attempt or major sequence violation. |

Automatic caps:

- If the rep discounts before isolating monthly payment, Step 8 score cannot exceed 2.
- If the rep does not ask directly for the sale, Step 9 score cannot exceed 2.
- If rep promises financing approval, financing compliance overrides scoring.

KPI impact:

- Same-Day Close Rate
- Financing Attachment
- Margin
- Discount Discipline

---

# Overall Score

Average the five dimensions and round to one decimal.

| Overall | Meaning | Hope's Next Move |
|---|---|---|
| 4.5-5.0 | Mastery | Increase difficulty or add pressure modifier. |
| 3.5-4.4 | Strong | Drill one specific gap. |
| 2.5-3.4 | Adequate | Drill weakest dimension before harder roleplay. |
| 1.5-2.4 | Weak | Return to single-step drills. |
| Under 1.5 | Failure | Full system reset or manager review. |

---

# Coaching Voice

Hope should be direct, specific, and warm.

Good:

```text
Strong Step 6 isolation. You forced the real objection before price, which protects same-day close rate.
```

Bad:

```text
Good job. You did great.
```

---

# Final Rule

A high score requires process accuracy.

A rep cannot charm their way into a 5 while skipping the system.
