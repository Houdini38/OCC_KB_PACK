# Single Step Roleplay Templates

## Purpose

Single Step Roleplay lets reps practice one specific step without running a full appointment.

Use this for fast repetitions, targeted correction, and word-track confidence.

---

# Setup Template

Before starting, Hope should confirm:

1. Step number
2. Persona
3. Product
4. Difficulty
5. Coaching preference

Opening:

> OK, we are practicing Step [#]: [Step Name]. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. Stay inside this step. I will score only this step unless you ask to continue. Start when ready.

---

# Step 1 - Warm-Up

## Start Point

The rep is at the homeowner's door or just inside the home.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: cooperative
- Medium: asks how long this will take
- Hard: says, "I just need a price"

## Pass Standard

Rep sets agenda, confirms appointment length, confirms decision-maker involvement, and transitions to inspection.

## KPI Focus

Process Compliance / Close Rate

---

# Step 2 - Pre-Assessment / Inspection

## Start Point

Rep and homeowner are walking the exterior.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: notices issues quickly
- Medium: downplays urgency
- Hard: argues that damage is minor

## Pass Standard

Rep involves homeowner, identifies issue, explains consequence, uses Past-Future-Present, and earns agreement that issue should be addressed.

## KPI Focus

Same-Day Close / Urgency

---

# Step 3 - Company Story / Challenger Method

## Start Point

Inspection is complete. Rep is transitioning to how the customer can solve the project.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: listens and agrees
- Medium: says they may use a local contractor
- Hard: says all contractors are the same

## Pass Standard

Rep teaches Three Ways to Do Business, tailors to the home, and differentiates SuperiorPRO without attacking competitors.

## KPI Focus

Close Rate / Shopping Resistance

---

# Step 4 - Investment / Price Conditioning

## Start Point

Company Story is complete. Rep is preparing the customer to think in cost-vs-value terms.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: agrees quality matters
- Medium: asks if this will be expensive
- Hard: says they are price shopping

## Pass Standard

Rep frames investment, explains cost-vs-value, avoids actual pricing, and transitions into demo.

## KPI Focus

Average Ticket / Close Rate

---

# Step 5 - Product Demonstration

## Start Point

Rep has samples, photos, or product explanation ready.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: interested in visuals
- Medium: asks what makes it different
- Hard: challenges specs or asks for competitor comparison

## Pass Standard

Rep makes value physical, explains complete project, connects to the home, avoids unsupported claims, and confirms understanding.

## KPI Focus

Average Ticket / Same-Day Close

---

# Step 6 - Pre-Close

## Start Point

Product demonstration is complete. Rep is about to clear non-price objections.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: agrees project makes sense
- Medium: hesitates around timing
- Hard: gives vague "we'll see" language

## Pass Standard

Rep confirms custom-order logic, when-not-if, and isolates anything other than investment.

## KPI Focus

Same-Day Close / Objection Isolation

---

# Step 7 - Paperwork / Scope Confirmation

## Start Point

Rep is preparing the scope before price.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: reviews details
- Medium: asks if items can be removed
- Hard: tries to strip scope to lower price before seeing price

## Pass Standard

Rep confirms materials, labor, options, exclusions, and complete scope before pricing.

## KPI Focus

Production Accuracy / Cancellation Reduction

---

# Step 8 - Price Presentation

## Start Point

Scope is confirmed. Rep is ready to present List Price.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: mild surprise
- Medium: payment concern
- Hard: says price is too high or asks for best price

## Pass Standard

Rep presents List Price, high starting monthly, asks how payment fits, isolates monthly comfort, confirms target, trial-closes, then offers Monthly Promotion.

## KPI Focus

Financing Attachment / Margin / Close Rate

---

# Step 9 - Closing Sequence

## Start Point

Customer has agreed it is worth it but more than they anticipated paying.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: cooperative
- Medium: hesitant
- Hard: resists same-day decision

## Pass Standard

Rep physically disarms, asks timeline question, uses WOW line, presents Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity, gives adjusted offer, and asks directly for the sale.

## KPI Focus

Same-Day Close / Margin

---

# Step 10 - Warm Down

## Start Point

Customer has said yes.

## Customer Behavior

- Easy: excited
- Medium: asks next-step questions
- Hard: shows mild buyer's remorse

## Pass Standard

Rep reinforces decision, confirms next steps, clarifies communication, and prevents buyer's remorse.

## KPI Focus

Cancellation Reduction / Reviews / Referrals

# Final Rule

Stay inside the selected step.

Single Step Roleplay is for repetition, not wandering into the whole appointment.
