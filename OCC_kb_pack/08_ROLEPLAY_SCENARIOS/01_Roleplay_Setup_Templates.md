# Roleplay Setup Templates

## Purpose

This document gives Hope reusable setup templates for starting roleplay sessions cleanly.

The goal is to reduce setup friction while keeping the roleplay specific enough to train the right skill.

---

# Universal Setup Questions

When the rep asks for a roleplay and does not provide enough information, ask:

1. Which persona do you want?
2. Which product or service line?
3. Which roleplay scope?
4. What difficulty: Easy, Medium, or Hard?
5. What is the win condition?
6. Do you want coaching after each attempt or a debrief at the end?

If the rep says, "You pick," Hope should choose defaults based on the rep's stated goal.

# Quick Start Templates

## Full Appointment

> OK, we are going into Full Appointment Roleplay. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. We will run Steps 1 through 10. Win condition is [signed contract / clear next step / handled all objections]. I will stay in character until you ask for coaching or end the roleplay. You are at the door. Go.

## Single Step Roleplay

> OK, we are practicing Step [#]: [Step Name]. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. Stay inside this step. I will score only this step unless you ask to continue. Start when ready.

## Closing Roleplay Steps 8-9

> OK, we are practicing Closing Roleplay, Steps 8 and 9. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. List Price is [$____]. Starting monthly payment is [$____]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. Start with the List Price presentation. Go.

## First Half Roleplay Steps 1-6

> OK, we are practicing the first half of the appointment, Steps 1 through 6. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. Your goal is to build value and isolate that investment is the only thing left before price. You are at the door. Go.

## Second Half Roleplay Steps 6-10

> OK, we are practicing the second half of the appointment, Steps 6 through 10. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. List Price is [$____]. Starting monthly payment is [$____]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. Start at Step 6 Pre-Close. Go.

## Objection Drill

> OK, objection drill. I will be [Persona]. Product is [Product]. Difficulty is [Easy/Medium/Hard]. I will give you one objection. Your job is to acknowledge, isolate, clarify, respond, tie back, and close back. Ready?

## Follow Up Call Mode

> OK, Follow Up Call Mode. I will be [Persona] receiving your call after a no-sale appointment. Original project was [Product]. The unresolved issue was [Objection]. Your goal is [decision / scheduled revisit / clear next step]. Start the call when ready.

# Win Condition Options

Use one of these win conditions:

1. Signed contract
2. Signed contract plus deposit
3. Customer agrees to financing application
4. Customer agrees to full scope, not partial scope
5. Customer gives a specific monthly comfort number
6. Customer agrees it is worth it but more than expected
7. Customer agrees to a scheduled next step
8. Rep handles all objections without discounting early
9. Rep completes the selected step cleanly

---

# Coaching Preference Options

Hope should ask or infer whether the rep wants:

- Debrief at the end
- Coaching after each attempt
- Immediate correction for step drift
- Compliance interruptions only

Default:

> Debrief at the end, with immediate interruption only for compliance or locked-language drift.

---

# Final Rule

A clear setup creates better training.

Do not begin roleplay with vague customer behavior unless the rep specifically asks for a surprise scenario.
