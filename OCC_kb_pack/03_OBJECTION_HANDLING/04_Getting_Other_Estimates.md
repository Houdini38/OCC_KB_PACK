# Getting Other Estimates

## Customer Says

- "We always get three prices."
- "We need to get other estimates."
- "We want to compare."
- "We have another contractor coming out."
- "Your price may be good, but we want to shop around."

## What It Usually Means

This usually means the customer still sees the decision as a price comparison instead of a risk, scope, value, and company comparison.

It may mean:

1. Step 3 did not differentiate SuperiorPRO strongly enough.
2. Step 4 did not condition cost versus value.
3. The customer does not understand the risk of partial or incomplete scope.
4. The rep did not make the complete project clear.
5. The customer is using shopping as a safe delay.

## Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System

Primary steps:

- Step 3 - Company Story / Challenger Method
- Step 4 - Investment / Price Conditioning
- Step 5 - Product Demonstration

## Approved Response Framework

### Acknowledge

> I understand. Comparing options is reasonable, especially on a project this important.

### Reframe

> The biggest thing I would caution you on is making sure you are comparing the same scope, materials, labor, prep, installation standards, and warranty boundaries. Otherwise, it may look like you are comparing prices when you are actually comparing different projects.

### Tie Back

> Earlier we talked about doing this once and doing it right. That is why the full scope matters so much.

### Isolate

> Other than wanting another price, is there anything about the project, SuperiorPRO, the timing, or the investment that would keep you from moving forward?

## Close-Back Question

> If you felt confident that this was the right scope, the right company, and the investment made sense, would you be comfortable moving forward today?

## KPI Impact

- Close Rate
- Shopping Resistance
- Same-Day Close Rate
- Average Ticket

## Do Not Say

- "Other contractors are cheaper because they cut corners."
- "They will do a bad job."
- "You do not need other estimates."
- "This price disappears if you compare."
- "We are the best" unless supported by approved documentation.

## AI Coach Note

If this objection shows up late, diagnose Step 3 and Step 4. The rep may not have reframed the decision before price.

## Practice Drill

Have the rep explain why comparing scope matters without attacking competitors.
