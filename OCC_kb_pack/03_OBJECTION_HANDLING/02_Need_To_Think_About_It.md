# Need To Think About It

## Customer Says

- "I need to think about it."
- "Let us sleep on it."
- "We need some time."
- "We will get back to you."
- "I do not make decisions this quickly."

## What It Usually Means

This is often not the real objection.

It may mean:

1. The customer still has an unresolved concern.
2. The customer does not want to say no directly.
3. The investment feels high.
4. The value is not strong enough yet.
5. A decision-maker issue was not handled earlier.
6. The rep skipped Step 6 objection isolation.

## Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System

Primary steps:

- Step 6 - Pre-Close
- Step 8 - Price Presentation
- Step 9 - Closing Sequence

If this happens before price, diagnose Step 6.

If this happens after price but before Sharp Angle agreement, stay in Step 8.

If this happens after the approved Step 9 close ask, isolate the final objection.

## Approved Response Framework

### Acknowledge

> I understand. A project like this is important, and you should feel clear about the decision.

### Isolate

> What specifically do you need to think through: the project, SuperiorPRO, the timing, or the investment?

### Clarify

> Tell me more about that. What part still feels unresolved?

### Respond

Respond to the real concern using the appropriate system step.

- Project concern: return to scope and complete project value.
- Company concern: return to Step 3 differentiation.
- Timing concern: return to Step 2 urgency.
- Investment concern: return to Step 8 payment-control sequence.

## Close-Back Question

> If we can solve that concern, are you comfortable moving forward today?

If they still will not decide:

> What would need to happen between now and then for you to feel comfortable making a decision?

## KPI Impact

- Same-Day Close Rate
- Objection Isolation
- Close Rate
- Rehash Close Rate

## Do Not Say

- "What is there to think about?"
- "This price is only good right now" unless approved policy supports it.
- "You are making a mistake."
- "Let me just drop the price."
- "My manager might approve something if you decide today."

## AI Coach Note

Do not teach reps to argue with "think about it." Teach them to isolate what the customer actually needs to think about.

If the rep accepts "think about it" without isolation, score objection handling no higher than 3.

## Practice Drill

Have the rep ask the isolation question five different ways without sounding frustrated or defensive.
