# Same Day Close Resistance

## Customer Says

- "We do not make decisions the same day."
- "We never buy on the first visit."
- "We need to wait."
- "We do not want to be pressured."
- "We will call you if we decide."

## What It Usually Means

This may mean the customer is protecting themselves from pressure, uncertainty, or regret.

It may also mean:

1. Step 6 did not isolate non-price objections.
2. Step 8 did not make affordability clear.
3. Step 9 did not reduce pressure before asking for the decision.
4. The customer does not see a reason to decide now.
5. The rep sounded too pushy or too desperate.

## Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System

Primary steps:

- Step 6 - Pre-Close
- Step 8 - Price Presentation
- Step 9 - Closing Sequence

## Approved Response Framework

### Acknowledge

> I understand. You should not feel pressured into a project like this.

### Isolate

> Other than not wanting to feel rushed, is there anything about the project, SuperiorPRO, the timing, or the investment that would keep you from moving forward?

### Reconnect

> My goal is not to pressure you. My goal is to make sure you have a clear decision based on the issue we saw, the scope we built, and the investment options we reviewed.

### Close or Next Step

> If everything is clear and the investment works, are you comfortable moving forward today, or is there a specific issue we still need to solve?

## KPI Impact

- Same-Day Close Rate
- Margin
- Close Rate
- Rehash Close Rate

## Do Not Say

- "Why would you wait?"
- "This is only good right now" unless approved policy supports it.
- "You are making this harder than it needs to be."
- "I need this today."
- "What if I just drop the price?"

## AI Coach Note

Same-day close resistance is not always a final objection. It often reveals weak earlier steps.

Diagnose whether the rep:

- Created urgency in Step 2
- Built value in Steps 3 through 5
- Isolated objections in Step 6
- Controlled affordability in Step 8
- Disarmed correctly in Step 9

## Practice Drill

Have the rep respond to "we do not make same-day decisions" without pressure and then isolate the real concern.
