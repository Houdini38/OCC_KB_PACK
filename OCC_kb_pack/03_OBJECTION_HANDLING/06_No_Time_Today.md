# No Time Today

## Customer Says

- "I do not have time for all this today."
- "Can you just give me the number?"
- "I thought this would be quick."
- "I have another appointment soon."
- "Can we skip to the estimate?"

## What It Usually Means

This usually means Step 1 expectation-setting was weak or missing.

It may mean:

1. The rep did not confirm appointment length.
2. The office did not clearly set expectations.
3. The homeowner thought this was a quick quote.
4. The rep has not earned permission to run the process.
5. The homeowner is testing whether the rep will skip steps.

## Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System

Primary step:

- Step 1 - Warm-Up

## Approved Response Framework

### Acknowledge

> I understand. I do not want to rush you or waste your time.

### Reset Expectations

> The reason the appointment takes time is because I need to inspect the home, confirm the scope, and show you what is included before giving you exact pricing.

### Protect the Process

> If I just gave you a number without doing that, it would not be accurate or useful.

### Ask for Decision

> Do you still have enough time to do this the right way today, or would it be better to reschedule when we can give the project the time it deserves?

## Close-Back / Next-Step Question

> Would you rather continue and do it right now, or schedule a better time where we can go through it properly?

## KPI Impact

- Process Compliance
- Appointment Completion Quality
- Close Rate
- Same-Day Close Rate

## Do Not Say

- "It will only take a few minutes" if that is not true.
- "I can skip the presentation."
- "I will just give you a quick ballpark."
- "This is how we have to do it" in a defensive tone.
- "You should have known it would take this long."

## AI Coach Note

If this objection appears, diagnose Step 1 first.

Coach the rep to set appointment flow and timing before inspection.

## Practice Drill

Have the rep reset expectations without sounding defensive and ask whether to continue or reschedule.
