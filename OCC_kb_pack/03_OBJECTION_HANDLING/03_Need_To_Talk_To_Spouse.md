# Need To Talk To Spouse

## Customer Says

- "I need to talk to my spouse."
- "My husband/wife needs to see this."
- "I cannot decide without them."
- "We make decisions together."
- "Let me run it by them."

## What It Usually Means

This usually means the decision-maker issue was not handled early enough.

It may mean:

1. The rep failed to confirm decision-makers in Step 1.
2. The customer is using the spouse as a safe delay.
3. The absent spouse has real influence.
4. The present homeowner is not sold enough to advocate for the project.
5. The rep failed to isolate non-price objections in Step 6.

## Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System

Primary steps:

- Step 1 - Warm-Up
- Step 6 - Pre-Close

This should be prevented early, not discovered for the first time at close.

## Approved Response Framework

### Acknowledge

> I understand. Most couples want to make decisions like this together.

### Isolate

> Other than needing to include them, is there anything about the project, SuperiorPRO, the timing, or the investment that would keep you from moving forward?

### Clarify Influence

> When you talk with them, what part do you think they will care about most?

### Create Next Step

> Would it make sense to schedule a time where both of you can review the project together, so I can answer questions directly instead of making you carry the whole presentation back to them?

## Close-Back Question

> If they were here and felt good about the project and investment, would you be comfortable moving forward?

## KPI Impact

- Decision-Maker Control
- Same-Day Close Rate
- Close Rate
- Rehash Close Rate

## Do Not Say

- "You can decide without them, right?"
- "Just sign and explain it later."
- "They will be fine with it."
- "Can you call them real quick?" in a pushy or dismissive way.
- "This deal is gone if they are not here" unless approved policy supports it.

## AI Coach Note

If this objection appears late, diagnose Step 1 first.

Coach the rep to prevent spouse objections by identifying decision-makers before the appointment moves into value and price.

## Practice Drill

Have the rep practice asking who else is involved in the final decision without sounding accusatory or awkward.
