# Not Ready

## Customer Says

- "We are not ready yet."
- "This is not the right time."
- "Maybe later."
- "We are going to wait."
- "We want to do this eventually."

## What It Usually Means

The customer may not feel enough urgency, or the rep may not have isolated what "not ready" actually means.

It may mean:

1. The problem does not feel urgent enough.
2. The customer is unsure about timing.
3. The investment feels uncomfortable.
4. The project is competing with other priorities.
5. The rep did not use Step 2 to connect issue to consequence.
6. The rep skipped Step 6 objection isolation.

## Where It Belongs in the 10-Step System

Primary steps:

- Step 2 - Pre-Assessment / Inspection
- Step 6 - Pre-Close

If this appears after price, also inspect Step 8 for payment discomfort.

## Approved Response Framework

### Acknowledge

> I understand. Timing matters, especially with a project like this.

### Isolate

> When you say you are not ready, is it the timing, the project itself, SuperiorPRO, or the investment that feels unresolved?

### Reconnect to Problem

> Earlier, we saw [specific issue]. The concern is not just what it looks like today. The concern is what happens if it continues to sit.

### Clarify Timing

> What would need to change for you to feel ready?

## Close-Back Question

> If the timing and investment made sense, would this be something you would want to move forward with now, or is there another concern we still need to solve?

## KPI Impact

- Same-Day Close Rate
- Close Rate

## Do Not Say

- "You need to do this now."
- "Waiting is a terrible decision."
- "The price will definitely go way up."
- "This offer disappears forever" unless approved policy supports it.
- "What if I discounted it?" before isolating the true issue.

## AI Coach Note

If the rep hears "not ready" and simply accepts it, diagnose Step 2 and Step 6.

Coach the rep to identify whether this is timing, investment, trust, scope, or urgency.

## Practice Drill

Have the rep isolate "not ready" into timing, project, company, or investment in under 30 seconds.
