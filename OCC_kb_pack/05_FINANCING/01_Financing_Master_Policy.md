# Financing Master Policy

## Purpose

This document defines how financing should be positioned, coached, and protected inside the OneCallCloser.ai knowledge base.

Financing is a buying tool, not a discount substitute.

The purpose of financing is to help qualified homeowners understand affordability after value, scope, and List Price have been established.

This document supports:

- Sales System Master
- Step 8 Price Presentation
- Approved Financing Presentation Language
- Current Financing Programs
- Prohibited Financing Statements

If this document conflicts with prohibited financing statements or confirmed current financing programs, those higher-authority files win.

---

# Core Financing Philosophy

Financing should help the customer answer:

> Can this project fit my budget in a way that makes sense?

Financing should not be used to cover for:

- Weak discovery
- Weak urgency
- Weak product demonstration
- Weak value building
- Weak scope explanation
- Premature price presentation
- Panic discounting

If the rep has not built enough value, financing will not save the appointment.

# Universal Financing Rules

1. Use only approved plan numbers and payment factors.
2. Always pair financing discussion with required disclosures.
3. Never promise approval.
4. Never promise a rate, term, monthly payment, or lender decision.
5. Never imply SuperiorPRO controls lender approval.
6. Always explain that the lender determines final approval and terms.
7. Do not quote unconfirmed financing details at the kitchen table.
8. Do not use financing as a substitute for value building.
9. Do not volunteer maximum discount authority.
10. Do not move to promotion until Step 8 payment isolation is complete or the customer clearly refuses financing.

---

# Step 8 Financing Rule

Financing belongs in Step 8 after List Price is presented.

The approved Step 8 order is:

1. Present List Price confidently.
2. Present a high starting monthly payment.
3. Ask how that payment fits the customer's budget.
4. If they react to total price, acknowledge briefly and redirect to monthly.
5. Isolate the customer's monthly comfort zone.
6. Confirm the monthly payment target.
7. Trial-close List Price.
8. Offer the Monthly Promotion.
9. Use Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle only if still needed.
10. If the customer refuses financing, verify that they can pay 35% down and the balance upon completion.

---

# When To Lead With Financing

Lead with monthly payment during Step 8 after List Price has been presented.

Use financing when:

- The full project investment feels large.
- The customer reacts emotionally to total price.
- The customer needs affordability options.
- The rep needs to isolate monthly comfort before offering promotion.
- The customer is open to payment options.

---

# When Not To Push Financing

Do not push financing when:

- The customer clearly confirms twice that they are a cash buyer.
- The customer does not consent to a credit application.
- The customer has a scope or value concern that has not been resolved.
- A hard credit pull would be inappropriate or unwanted.
- The customer has already clearly declined financing and can pay the required deposit and balance.

In those cases, respect the preference and continue the sales process without inventing financing pressure.

---

# Discount Authority Rules

Discount authority is internal and must not be volunteered to the customer.

Rules:

1. Never volunteer the maximum discount.
2. Use Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle before additional discounting.
3. The First Drop must be justified through approved Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity language in Step 9.
4. Discounts beyond the listed plan maximum require manager approval.
5. Discount maximums vary by plan.
6. Plan 6160 carries 0% discount allowance.
7. If the homeowner needs both a 0% APR plan and a discount, choose an approved plan that allows discount room.

Exact maximum discount allowances are controlled by the Current Financing Programs file.

---

# Plan 6160 Guardrail

Plan 6160 is a 0% APR for 60 months plan.

It carries 0% max discount off List Price.

Do not recommend Plan 6160 together with a discount request.

If the homeowner needs both a lower payment and a discount, recommend a different approved plan with discount allowance.

# Hope Coaching Triggers

Hope must flag these errors:

- Rep mentions financing only after the customer objects to total price.
- Rep skips required disclosure language.
- Rep promises approval.
- Rep promises a specific rate, term, or monthly payment.
- Rep says "free financing" or "guaranteed approval."
- Rep does not isolate monthly comfort before presenting the promotion offer.
- Rep volunteers a discount before isolating monthly comfort.
- Rep offers a discount above the plan maximum without manager approval.
- Rep recommends Plan 6160 with a discount request.
- Rep uses Step 9 Promo Home language inside Step 8.

# Missing-Information Rule

If the rep or customer asks about a financing detail that is not confirmed in the KB, use this response:

For AI coaching, use:
