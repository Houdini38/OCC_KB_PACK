# Prohibited Financing Statements

## Purpose

This document defines prohibited financing claims and required financing disclosures.

This is a Tier 1 compliance document.

Compliance always wins.

The AI coach must interrupt any roleplay, coaching session, demonstration, follow-up call, or transcript review where these claims are made.

---

# Why This Document Exists

GreenSky® consumer loans are made by Synovus Bank, Member FDIC, NMLS #408043.

SuperiorPRO is not the lender.

All credit decisions, rates, and terms are determined by program lenders.

Misrepresenting financing can create legal, lender-relationship, customer-trust, and compliance risk.

---

# Required Compliance Interrupt Protocol

When the AI coach detects a prohibited financing claim, it must immediately interrupt and use this structure:

```text
Stop. That language is not approved.
Issue:
Why it matters:
Approved replacement:
Restart from:
```

## Example

```text
Stop. That language is not approved.
Issue: You implied the customer would be approved for financing.
Why it matters: SuperiorPRO is not the lender. The lender makes approval and term decisions.
Approved replacement: We can submit the application and see what you qualify for. The lender makes the final decision.
Restart from the payment explanation.
```

# Category 1 - Approval Guarantees

## Banned Phrases

Do not say:

- "You're guaranteed to be approved."
- "Everyone gets approved."
- "Your credit is fine, no problem."
- "I can get you approved."
- "You will qualify."
- "Approval will not be an issue."

## Approved Replacements

- "We can submit the application and see what you qualify for."
- "The lender will make the final decision based on the application."
- "Financing is available for qualified customers."

## Why It Is Banned

SuperiorPRO does not approve loans.

Promising approval misrepresents the lender relationship.

---

# Category 2 - Rate or Term Promises Outside Lender Control

## Banned Phrases

Do not say:

- "You will get the lowest rate."
- "Your rate will be ____."
- "I can lock in this rate for you."
- "You will definitely get ____ months."
- "Your payment will be exactly ____."
- "This plan is guaranteed."

## Approved Replacements

- "The lender determines the specific rate and terms based on the application."
- "We can calculate an estimated payment using the approved payment factor, but the lender confirms final terms."
- "Your specific APR within this range is determined by the lender based on your application."

---

# Category 3 - Free Financing or No-Cost Financing Language

## Banned Phrases

Do not say:

- "Free financing."
- "No-cost financing."
- "Free money."
- "There's no catch."
- "We are giving you the loan."

## Approved Direction

Use accurate plan language.

Approved examples:

- "0% APR financing" when the approved plan is truly 0% APR.
- "Interest is waived if the full amount is paid before the promotional period ends" for deferred-interest plans.
- "Let me explain how the plan works" instead of "there's no catch."

---

# Category 4 - Misrepresenting Deferred Interest

Deferred-interest plans accrue interest during the promotional period. Interest is waived only if the full balance is paid before the promotional period ends.

## Banned Phrases

Do not say:

- "No interest for 18 months" without the required waiver disclosure.
- "You will not pay any interest" without the payoff condition.
- "It is basically a 0% loan" when it is deferred interest.
- "Just pay it off whenever."

## Required Disclosure for Deferred-Interest Plans

> Interest is waived if the full amount is paid before the promotional period ends. If not paid in full, interest that accrued during the promo period will be charged.

## Required Disclosure for Deferred Interest With Payments

> Monthly payments are required during and after the promotional period. Interest is waived only if the full balance is paid before the promo period ends.

---

# Category 5 - Implying SuperiorPRO Is The Lender

## Banned Phrases

Do not say:

- "We finance you."
- "Our financing department approves you."
- "We approve everyone with decent credit."
- "We can adjust your rate."
- "SuperiorPRO controls the financing terms."

## Approved Replacement

> We work with GreenSky for financing. The loans are made by Synovus Bank, and the lender makes all credit decisions and determines the specific rate and terms.

---

# Category 6 - Bypassing Required Disclosures

Reps may never skip required disclosures during financing presentations.

## Required for All Financing Presentations

> Financing is provided through GreenSky and made by Synovus Bank. The lender makes all credit decisions and determines your specific rate and terms.

## Required for Deferred-Interest Plans

> Interest is waived if the full amount is paid before the promotional period ends. If not paid in full, interest that accrued during the promo period will be charged.

## Required for Deferred Interest With Payments

> Monthly payments are required during and after the promotional period. Interest is waived only if the full balance is paid before the promo period ends.

## Required for Range-Rate Plans

> Your specific APR within this range is determined by the lender based on your application.

## Required Legal Disclosure

> GreenSky® program consumer loans are made by Synovus Bank, Member FDIC, NMLS #408043.

---

# Category 7 - Credit Pull Language

Do not say a credit pull has no effect on the customer's credit unless that exact language is confirmed by approved lender documentation.

If not confirmed, do not say it.

Use:

> Let me confirm the exact application language before I quote that.

---

# Category 8 - Application Pressure

The customer controls consent.

Do not pressure, mislead, or coerce a customer into a financing application.

Do not say:

- "You have to apply to get this deal."
- "There is no downside to applying" unless confirmed by approved lender documentation.
- "Worst case, you just have another number" if it minimizes credit or application implications.

Approved direction:

> If you would like to see what you qualify for, we can submit the application and the lender will determine approval and terms.

---

# Step 8 Financing Compliance Guardrails

The AI coach must correct the rep if they:

- Offer promotion before isolating monthly payment.
- Promise financing approval.
- Promise a specific rate, term, or payment not confirmed by lender decision.
- Invent a payment amount without using the approved payment factor.
- Skip required financing disclosures.
- Use Step 9 Promo Home language inside Step 8.
- Use Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle before the Monthly Promotion.
- Fail to verify 35% deposit ability when the customer refuses financing.

---

# Follow-Up Call Financing Guardrails

Follow-up call language can be flexible, but financing facts cannot be flexible.

In Follow Up Call Mode, the AI coach must correct the rep if they:

- Offers a new financing promise not confirmed in the KB.
- Says the customer will qualify.
- Offers a new discount or promotion without authority.
- Reframes Step 9 Promo Home language as a fresh callback offer unless approved by manager policy.
- Pressures the customer into applying.

# Escalation Path

If a rep repeatedly uses prohibited financing language:

1. First instance: correct in real time.
2. Second instance in the same session: note it in the debrief.
3. Pattern across sessions: recommend focused financing compliance retraining.
4. Severe customer-facing violation: recommend manager review before additional solo appointments.

---

# Final Rule

Financing compliance is not optional.

A clean compliant financing explanation protects the customer, the rep, SuperiorPRO, GreenSky relationship, and the sale.
