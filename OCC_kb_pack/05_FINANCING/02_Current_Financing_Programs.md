# Guardrail

Never invent, estimate, or paraphrase financing terms at the kitchen table.

Use only the plan numbers, rates, terms, payment factors, and disclosures listed in this file.

If a homeowner asks about a plan not listed here, say:

> I want to make sure I give you the exact number. Let me confirm that and follow up.

Do not guarantee approval. GreenSky® makes all credit decisions through program lenders.

All listed plans have a 6-month purchase window.

# How To Calculate Monthly Payment

Formula:

```text
Project Amount x Payment Factor = Monthly Payment
```

Use this calculation only as an estimate unless lender terms are confirmed.

## Examples Using a $25,000 Project

| Plan | Factor | Monthly Payment Estimate |
|---|---:|---:|
| 6124 | 4.17% | ~$1,043 |
| 6136 | 2.78% | ~$695 |
| 6148 | 2.08% | ~$520 |
| 6160 | 1.67% | ~$418 |
| 2716 | 1.98% | ~$495 |
| 2832 | 1.49% | ~$373 |
| 9998 | 0.96% | ~$240 |

Do not present the estimate as guaranteed approval or guaranteed final payment.

# Step 8 Financing Sequence Notes

1. Always present List Price first to anchor value.
2. Lead with a high starting monthly payment using at least a 3% payment factor off List Price.
3. Ask: "How does $X per month fit into your budget?"
4. Isolate the monthly comfort zone before offering any promotion or adjusted plan.
5. Match a plan to their number by presenting the option that gets closest to their stated range.
6. Never promise approval. GreenSky program lenders make credit decisions.
7. Do not invent payment amounts. Calculate using the approved payment factors above.

# Discount Authority Awareness

The Max Discount off List Price column shows the maximum discount allowed for each financing plan.

This is internal guidance and should not be volunteered to the homeowner.

Rules:

- Never volunteer the maximum discount.
- Use Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle before additional discounting.
- First drop must be justified through approved Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity language.
- Discounts beyond the listed max require manager approval.
- Plan 6160 carries 0% discount allowance.

## Highest Discount Authority

Plans 2511, 2720, 2832, and 9991 allow up to 25% max discount off List Price.

## Lowest Discount Authority

Plan 6160 allows 0% max discount off List Price.
