# Overview

SuperiorPRO installs and repairs traditional stucco and synthetic stucco / EIFS-style systems where appropriate.

Stucco should be positioned around moisture management, proper diagnosis, correct repair method, texture/color blending, and long-term wall-system performance.

Atlanta-area stucco work must account for:

- Moisture intrusion
- Cracking
- Settlement
- Drainage issues
- Improper prior repairs
- Substrate condition
- Texture and color matching limits

# Homeowner-Facing New Stucco Process

Use this as a general explanation. Actual scope may vary by contract.

1. Inspect substrate, sheathing, framing, and visible damage.
2. Identify moisture or drainage concerns.
3. Install moisture-management components according to project scope.
4. Install lath or reinforcement where required.
5. Apply base coats / leveling coats according to system requirements.
6. Apply finish coat, texture, and color according to scope.
7. Allow appropriate cure time.
8. Complete final inspection and walk-through.

---

# Homeowner-Facing Stucco Repair Process

Use this as a general explanation. Actual scope may vary by contract.

1. Identify the cause of the problem: cracking, water intrusion, impact, settling, or prior repair failure.
2. Remove damaged stucco back to sound material where included.
3. Inspect the substrate and repair damaged areas according to scope.
4. Rebuild the moisture barrier / repair zone as needed.
5. Re-lath or reinforce as required.
6. Apply repair coats and finish texture.
7. Blend color as closely as practical.
8. Explain limitations on matching aged stucco.

# Why It Is Different From Cheap Stucco Repairs

Use category comparisons, not attacks.

Cheap or incomplete repairs may fail because they:

- Patch over the symptom without identifying the cause.
- Paint over cracks instead of repairing them.
- Ignore moisture intrusion.
- Skip drainage or weep details.
- Apply new material over failing material.
- Promise perfect color matching on aged stucco.

Approved language:

> With stucco, the surface crack is often just the clue. The real question is whether there is movement, moisture, or failure behind it. That is why diagnosis matters before repair.

# Top Customer Questions

## Customer: "Why does my stucco have cracks?"

Approved response:

> Hairline cracking can happen as a home moves or settles. Larger cracks, staining, softness, or recurring cracks can point to moisture or movement issues. That is what we inspect before recommending a repair.

## Customer: "Can you just patch it?"

Approved response:

> Sometimes, yes. But only if the underlying cause is not active. If water or movement is causing the failure, patching the surface may only hide the problem temporarily.

## Customer: "Traditional stucco or EIFS - which is better?"

Approved response:

> Both can work when installed correctly. The right recommendation depends on the existing system, the damage, moisture concerns, and the project goal. We inspect first, then recommend the right repair or system.

## Customer: "Can you paint stucco?"

Approved response:

> Yes, stucco can be painted with the right prep and coating system. If painting is part of the project, we coordinate that scope carefully because moisture and surface condition matter.

## Customer: "Can you match the color exactly?"

Approved response:

> We can aim for a close match, but exact color matching on aged stucco can be limited. We set that expectation before work begins.

## Customer: "What warranty do I get?"

Approved response:

> Warranty depends on the product and written warranty documents. SuperiorPRO generally covers installation issues for 24 months, and manufacturer coverage may apply depending on the product.
