# Overview

SuperiorPRO installs seamless aluminum gutters and gutter guard systems.

Gutters and guards should be positioned around water control, fascia protection, foundation risk reduction, debris management, safety, and long-term maintenance reduction.

In Atlanta, gutter performance is heavily affected by:

- Heavy rain
- Tree debris
- Pine needles
- Shingle grit
- Roof pitch
- Downspout placement
- Fascia condition
- Poor pitch or sagging gutters

# Homeowner-Facing Install Process

Use this as a general explanation. Actual scope may vary by contract.

1. Remove old gutters and downspouts where included.
2. Inspect fascia for rot or damage.
3. Address fascia repairs according to the project scope.
4. Run seamless gutters on-site to fit the home.
5. Install hangers and secure gutters.
6. Install downspouts and route water flow.
7. Install gutter guards where included.
8. Test water flow where practical and complete walk-through.

# Why It Beats DIY / Big Box

Use category comparisons, not attacks.

Professional gutter installation can be positioned against DIY by explaining:

- Sectioned gutters create more joints and potential leak points.
- Incorrect pitch can cause standing water or overflow.
- Weak fastening can lead to sagging.
- Poor downspout placement can dump water in the wrong place.
- Ladder work creates homeowner safety risk.

Approved language:

> Gutters look simple, but performance comes from pitch, hangers, downspout routing, fascia condition, and a clean install. If any of those are wrong, the system can overflow even if the gutter itself is new.

# Top Customer Questions

## Customer: "How often do I clean them?"

Approved response:

> Gutter guards reduce debris buildup, but they do not eliminate maintenance completely. Cleaning needs depend on tree coverage, roof debris, and conditions around the home.

## Customer: "Will guards stop ice dams?"

Approved response:

> Guards can help keep gutters draining, but ice dams can also be affected by attic insulation, ventilation, roof conditions, and weather. I would not position guards as a complete ice-dam solution.

## Customer: "What size do I need?"

Approved response:

> Size depends on roof area, pitch, water volume, and layout. We inspect the home and recommend the size that fits the project.

## Customer: "Can I add guards to existing gutters?"

Approved response:

> Often that may be possible, but we need to inspect the existing gutters first. If they are sagging, damaged, or poorly pitched, replacement may be the better long-term answer.

## Customer: "What warranty do I get?"

Approved response:

> Warranty depends on the product and written warranty documents. SuperiorPRO generally covers installation issues for 24 months, and manufacturer coverage may apply depending on the product.
