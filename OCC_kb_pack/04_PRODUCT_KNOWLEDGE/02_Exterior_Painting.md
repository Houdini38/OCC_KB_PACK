# Overview

SuperiorPRO installs Sherwin-Williams exterior paint systems.

Exterior painting should be positioned as more than color. A professional exterior paint project is a protection system that depends heavily on prep, repair, primer, application method, and weather timing.

Atlanta-area exterior painting must account for:

- Humidity
- UV exposure
- Temperature swings
- Moisture intrusion
- Wood rot risk
- Surface failure
- Mildew and dirt buildup

# Homeowner-Facing Install Process

Use this as a general explanation of the complete project. Actual scope may vary by contract.

1. Power wash the exterior where included.
2. Scrape, sand, and prep loose or peeling areas.
3. Identify and address damaged or rotted wood according to the project scope.
4. Caulk gaps where included and appropriate.
5. Spot prime bare areas as needed.
6. Apply finish coats according to the written scope.
7. Complete trim, doors, and accent areas where included.
8. Perform walk-through and touch-up.

# Why It Beats DIY

Professional exterior painting can be positioned against DIY by explaining:

- Prep is labor-heavy and easy to skip.
- Exterior painting requires ladders, safety, and weather awareness.
- Application consistency affects appearance and durability.
- Surface repair matters before paint is applied.
- Homeowners often underestimate the time required.

---

# Why It Beats Cheap Painters

Use category comparisons, not attacks.

A cheap paint job may cost less because of:

- Minimal prep
- Fewer coats
- Lower-grade product
- Rushed application
- Weak caulking or repair scope
- No clear documentation of what is included

Approved language:

> A low paint price usually means something is missing. The first thing to check is prep, product, number of coats, wood repair, caulking, cleanup, and what happens if the coating fails.

# Top Customer Questions

## Customer: "How long will it last?"

Approved response:

> Lifespan depends on the product, prep, surface condition, sun exposure, moisture, and maintenance. The biggest factor we can control is doing the prep and application correctly.

## Customer: "What if I have wood rot?"

Approved response:

> We identify damaged wood during inspection. If it is part of the included scope, we document it. If it is more than the original scope, we review it with you before moving forward.

## Customer: "What colors do I get?"

Approved response:

> Sherwin-Williams has a large color library. We will help you select colors that fit the home and the project scope.

## Customer: "Do I need to be home during the work?"

Approved response:

> Not necessarily, but a walk-through at the start and end is helpful. We coordinate access, timing, and communication before work begins.

## Customer: "What happens if it rains?"

Approved response:

> Weather matters. We monitor conditions and adjust work timing so the application is not compromised.

## Customer: "What warranty do I get?"

Approved response:

> Warranty depends on the product and written warranty documents. SuperiorPRO generally covers installation issues for 24 months, and manufacturer coverage may apply depending on the product.
