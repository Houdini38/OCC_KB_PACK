# Close Ask Language

## Purpose

This file controls approved language for Step 9 - Closing Sequence.

Step 9 converts the customer's "worth it" agreement into a same-day decision using the approved close sequence.

This file supports the Sales System Master. If this file conflicts with compliance, compliance wins. If this file conflicts with Step 8, keep the steps separate and follow the Conflict Priority Hierarchy.

---

# Step 9 Core Rule

Step 9 closes the decision.

Step 9 begins after the customer agrees the project is worth it but more than anticipated.

Do not use Step 8 payment-isolation language inside Step 9.

Do not place Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle inside Step 9. It belongs in Step 8 before Step 9 begins.

---

# Approved Step 9 Process Flow

1. Begin immediately after the Sharp Angle agreement.
2. Physically disarm: sit back, put the pen down, begin stacking papers, relax posture.
3. Ask the timeline question.
4. Deliver the WOW line no matter what timeline they give.
5. Introduce the Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity as a justified First Drop.
6. Present the adjusted offer.
7. Ask for the sale directly.
8. If they still hesitate, isolate the final objection and do not stack unapproved discounts.

---

# Approved Word Track - Three Parts

## Part 1 - Disarm and Timeline

> [Physically disarm.] How soon do you think you would be able to get back with me?

## Part 2 - WOW Line and Justified First Drop

> Wow, you must be pretty motivated to get this project done. I tell you what, It is my job to keep orders flowing into the warehouse and while I’d love to get your business next week the truth is, I need to earn it today. I do have something that might help both of us out. All I ask is that you keep an open mind, FAIR ENOUGH???. Great, our marketing dept. has set aside some marketing money instead of spending it on TV and radio. We use it as a special incentive for homeowners who are ready to move forward. Let me show you what that would look like.

## Part 3 - Direct Close and Final Objection Control

> Based on this adjusted offer, are you comfortable moving forward with the project today?

If they hesitate:

> I understand. What specifically is holding you back at this point: the project, the company, the timing, or the investment?

---

# Required Moves / Checkpoints

- Physical disarm before the timeline question.
- Required line: "Wow, you must be pretty motivated to get this project done."
- Required ask: "Based on this adjusted offer, are you comfortable moving forward with the project today?"

---

# What The Rep Must Accomplish

The rep must:

1. Reduce pressure through physical disarm.
2. Get the customer to state or imply timeline.
3. Use the WOW line.
4. Justify the First Drop.
5. Present the adjusted offer cleanly.
6. Ask directly for the sale.
7. Isolate any final objection.
8. Avoid stacking unapproved discounts.

---

# What Not To Say

Do not say:

- "Let me see what I can do."
- "My manager might approve this."
- "You twisted my arm."
- "This is only good for ten minutes" unless approved policy supports it.
- "I can make this happen" if the rep does not have authority.
- "What if I dropped the price again?"
- "I do not want to lose your business" in a desperate way.

---

# Drift Watch

Do not blend Step 9 with Step 8.

Step 9 must not own:

- Monthly payment isolation
- Payment target confirmation
- List Price trial close
- Add/Subtract/Sharp Angle

Those belong to Step 8.

Step 9 owns:

- Physical disarm
- Timeline question
- WOW line
- Promo Home / Marketing Opportunity
- Adjusted offer
- Direct close
- Final objection isolation

---

# Compliance Guardrail

The First Drop must be justified and accurate.

The rep must not create fake scarcity, fake manager approval, or unapproved discount authority.

If the rep misrepresents the promotion or discount authority, Hope must interrupt and correct the language.

---

# KPI Impact

Step 9 affects:

- Same-Day Close Rate
- Margin
- Discount Discipline
- Final Objection Control

Weak Step 9 creates delayed decisions and unnecessary rehash.
